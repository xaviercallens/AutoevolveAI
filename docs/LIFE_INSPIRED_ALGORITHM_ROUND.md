## 1. The candidate: OPERON-minus-glue

Keep only the one claim that held up: **for a Lean module compiled standalone against a frozen spec, the verdict of `verify_against_spec` is a deterministic function of (module source bytes, spec sha256, toolchain string, lake-manifest sha, sorted obligation names) and of nothing else in the genome.** That is a genuine repair of Paper H's defect — the certificate rides on the bytes that are copied, rather than being computed from the whole program at evaluation time — and it uses no four-point condition, no split decomposition, no behavioural-profile alignment.

Say plainly what that makes it: **content-addressed memoisation of a deterministic verifier, plus a representation constraint that makes the unit of variation coincide with the unit of caching.** It is a build cache. The integron analogy earns exactly one design decision — a cassette's contract is with shared machinery, not with its neighbour, which is why pairwise glue must be deleted — and nothing else. It is not a new life-inspired computing algorithm, and should not be written up as one.

## 2. The algorithm

**Representation.** Genome = ordered list of M slots + a sha-pinned frozen spec S. Slot = `(index, python_source, lean_source, obligation_map, content_sha)`, slot *i* declaring `namespace ANSE.Op.Slot{i}`. Closure invariant, enforced by the elaborator in a standalone compile: no sibling import, no sibling-qualified identifier, declared names globally unique across slots (not merely namespaced — `_lookup_axioms` matches by unqualified suffix). **No glue genes.** Each slot's interface obligation is stated against the frozen spec's own namespace; composition is proved once as a spec theorem and cached permanently.

**Cache.** `results/operon/verdict_cache.sqlite`. Key = sha256(source ‖ spec_sha256 ‖ toolchain_string ‖ lake_manifest_sha ‖ sorted(obligation_names)). **PASS verdicts only.** A non-pass is never cached, because `rc != 0` covers compile timeout (-1), forgery-scan hit (-2), OOM and a missing `#print axioms` row — apparatus and bookkeeping refusals indistinguishable from data refusals at exactly the point where a verdict would become heritable.

**Variation** (structure RNG): (a) slot crossover, slots i..j from A, rest from B — all modules verbatim, zero novel keys; (b) slot mutation by a **new** Lean-source mutator (the existing `ASTGeneticOperators` is over Python's `ast` and cannot be reused — this is new, uncosted component); (c) duplication-and-divergence with canonical alpha-renaming before hashing, otherwise a mathematically identical duplicate always misses; (d) slot deletion. Noise RNG (separate stream): constant jitter and tie-breaking.

**Selection.** Unchanged MAP-Elites / NSGA-II / epsilon-lexicase. Do **not** adopt `discharged_local_obligations` as the second axis: acceptance requires all M obligations to pass, so it is identically M and as degenerate as the `memory_mb` it replaces (`compute_cell`:819 bins `memory_mb // 2.0`, and healthy kernels report 1.0–2.4, so `m_bin` ∈ {0,1}). Measure `memory_mb` in the evaluator instead.

**Loop / gate.** Per candidate: compute keys → look up → compile each **missing module standalone** against the frozen spec with the unmodified `verify_against_spec` → accept iff all M obligations pass with axioms ⊆ {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound} → evaluate empirically only if accepted → append one row per compile.

## 3. The efficiency claim, as one falsifiable sentence

> At M = 8 slots and c = 7 ring-closable theorems per slot, the median Lean wall-clock seconds per candidate verdict under per-module content-addressed caching is ≤ 0.60× the median under **whole-candidate-file** content-addressed caching (≥ 1.65×), over ≥ 30 candidates replayed in identical order on a warm host, with the bootstrap 95% CI of the paired median ratio excluding 1.0 and the ratio exceeding the P90 of the measured same-input noise-ratio distribution.

Exact integers first (G6), before any ratio: Lean subprocess invocations; obligations elaborated; candidates evaluated; distinct module contents admitted; distinct whole-candidate files admitted; distinct Python-source hashes; cache hits/misses; re-queued compiles. Then seconds, one row per compile with file sha, spec sha, toolchain, rc, and per-obligation axiom list.

**Matched baseline: whole-candidate-file content-addressed cache.** Not no-cache (its compile count is exactly its candidate count by construction and need never be run), and not Python-source SHA — that was the key choice that manufactured SynDrift's entire headline.

Surviving mechanism, stated honestly: the lever is **incrementality** (recompile one novel module, not all M), measured at ≈ 2.1–2.4× at M=8 and 3.3–3.6× at M=16 across the two screening rounds. It is not φ, the dial the proposal called gameable, and φ should not be pre-registered as a gate. Note that 61% of a one-module miss (16.9 s of 27.5 s) is re-elaborating the concatenated frozen spec, so most of the claimed win may be available from a build-system fix with no evolutionary layer at all — see §5.

## 4. The experiment that would falsify it

**Arms.** (A) per-module cache. (B) whole-candidate-file cache — the floor, and the comparator. (C) no cache — not run; its count is an identity. (D) **constant-key cache** — the negative control: every lookup hits, so the "wrong coefficient" positive control must flip to `passed=True`. The nonce-key control from the proposal must **not** be used: its gating conditions (hit rate 0.000, compiles == candidates) are provable a priori from a 128-bit key, and the only clause that could fail is a 1.06 s margin against a 1.3–4× measured spread on byte-identical input.

**Realised-nuisance match.** By identity: A and B replay the same `trajectory.jsonl` candidate sequence, same order, same obligation set, same host, same warm Mathlib, one warm-up compile discarded per arm (66.9 s cold vs 16.9 s warm, measured). Reported as computed fields: novel modules per candidate, obligations per candidate, realised failure rate p_f, and all three distinct-content counts. Spread of the paired differences reported; zero spread ⇒ BLOCKED, not a p-value.

**Floor convergence (G4).** The floor was misidentified twice in this round, so it must be *shown*: from one run, report the hit rate at all three key granularities (Python-source sha, generated-file sha, per-module sha) and the resulting compile count at each. The claim is made only against the cheapest key, and the enumeration is published.

**Positive controls.** The four existing cases re-run at the **real 7-obligation map** (never yet done). Plus (7) a module proving its obligation via a sibling slot's lemma ⇒ REJECTED, binding clause "unknown identifier" (data). Plus (8) the same module in a shared environment ⇒ also REJECTED. Plus (9) a self-hijack: a module declaring a `notation` or `instance` that makes its mapped spec proposition trivially provable ⇒ REJECTED (currently uncaught; `detect_lean_source_forgeries` rejects only a literal `: True :=`).

**Pre-registered kill.** Any one of: paired median ratio at M=8 < 1.65×; its bootstrap CI includes 1.0; the ratio does not exceed the P90 noise ratio; measured speedup at M=16 not greater than at M=8; or ≥ 1 PASS-verdict or axiom-list disagreement in 200 standalone re-verifications of cached entries.

## 5. What not to build yet, and the smallest first step

2. **Spec-as-import vs spec-as-concatenated-body.** Compile a candidate against a precompiled `ANSE.VerletSymplectic` olean imported as a lake module, versus the current concatenation (`verify_against_spec` inlines the spec body into every audit file, so every miss re-elaborates it: 16.9 s of a 27.5 s miss).

If (1) shows median(monolith M=8) / median(one-module miss) does not exceed the P90 noise ratio, incrementality is UNRESOLVED and nothing further is built.

## 6. Honest scope

**Would establish, at most:** that content-addressed memoisation of a deterministic verifier at module granularity costs some measured factor less Lean wall-clock than whole-file memoisation, on one host, one toolchain (`v4.34.0-rc2`), and one 7-theorem spec whose goals all close by `ring` at sub-second marginal cost.

**Would not establish:** that recombination helps (fact 2 measured it buying nothing on the one task tested, identical best individual in both arms); that verification is the bottleneck at all (the engine currently performs zero per-child verification, so this is a discount on a new expense); that per-module credit assignment exists — **fitness remains whole-pipeline `total_energy`, which is Paper H's failure shape exactly, and is the precise place where this claim could silently drift back into the refuted design**; that any constant transfers to `linarith`/`nlinarith` goals over the reals, where content cost would exceed fixed overhead and the arithmetic of the claim changes; or that this is a life-inspired algorithm.

**Guards still unsatisfied.** G1 — no deliberately broken candidate exists in the closure dimension (controls 7–9 are specified, not built). G3 — satisfied by identity for the cost claim only; the paired trajectory is one the floor arm would never generate, so the claim is conditional on a recombination-heavy trajectory, and no search claim is matched at all. G4 — the floor was misidentified twice and its convergence is not yet shown. G5 — form is right (bootstrap over paired differences), resolution unknown until step 1 runs. G6 — satisfiable, not yet done; compiles per candidate must be reported before any ratio. G7 — **failing now**: the two load-bearing constants disagree by ~30% between rounds and no committed timing artifact exists. G8 — failing until the negative control becomes the constant-key cache. G9 — conjunct count is M after glue deletion, validated nowhere, and the gate's `defeq_checked >= 1` test is a live hole against a 7-theorem spec; the stored 4/4 artifact proves the 7-obligation path has never run. G10 / G11 — specified, not implemented (the engine has a single `rng`). G12 — **failing by construction** unless PASS-only caching is adopted, because `rc != 0` conflates timeout, forgery hit and missing axiom rows with a real refutation at exactly the point where a verdict becomes heritable.
