**Lessons today.**
- Audits keep finding real things: an outward-rounding bug (E2), a misread uncertainty widening (K2), a false
  statement of a theorem for non-strict maxima (K3), a misattributed quotation (LR-IND, earlier), a novelty claim
  killed by a prior abstract (EG-CB68).
- Two items turned out not to be new at all. That is a result: it is published as a recomputation, not hidden.
- The bundler has no allow-list. Every publication goes through a staging tree; metadata links must be verified, not
  guessed (a guessed URL redirecting to a portal was removed before publishing).
- Producer-written checkers are not independent of each other. The audits that wrote a third checker or a different
  algorithm were the valuable ones.
