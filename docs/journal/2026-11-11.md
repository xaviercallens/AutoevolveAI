## GE-2: certified partial census of bistability, three-species open bimolecular networks
- PUBLISHED: 10.5281/zenodo.24131124 (Tier C for witnesses, exclusions, enumeration; Tier X for the rest). Producer Sonnet, one independent Opus audit (ACCEPT_WITH_CORRECTIONS, applied), no human audit.
- Class: 3 species, complexes of molecularity <= 2, at most 3 internal reactions, inflow and linear removal for every species. 62,269 labelled networks, 10,523 up to relabelling.
- 754 with an exact rational bistability witness (all re-verified by the auditor's own checker); 5,071 excluded by a determinant-sign criterion (sampled re-derivation by the auditor); 807 multistationary-only (NOT independently verified); 3,891 UNDECIDED (37%).
- 9 bistable networks with two internal reactions; 175 minimal in the connected bucket, of which 79 are effectively two-species cascades and 96 irreducible by one screen. At least 57 more networks are bistable by inheritance (Joshi-Shiu), not counted.
- No claim of new mechanisms; no biological claim. The two-species classification (27 of 681) is Peng's (arXiv:2610.32739); our run of it is a control.
- Deviations from the preregistration: one wrong negative control dropped; a sampling defect fixed with a rerun; stage 2 not run.
