## Ranking

### 1. SLR-LOSS: certified loneliness records for n = 95..~140 (certified number, Lean-checkable)
Statement: for each n in a range, an explicit velocity set (Poliakova-type rule plus CP-SAT re-optimisation of rephasings, as in `SLR-EXT/code/cpsat_rule.py`) and an exact rational gamma_n = max_t min_i ||v_i t - s_i|| as small as possible, i.e. a certified upper bound on the shifted-loneliness E_n; compare with Poliakova's quantitative bound. Why new (candidate): Poliakova gives a rule with radius 1/(N+1); our n=63 result showed the open-radius slack (1/N) and sporadic moves can beat the rule's constants. Whether an optimised table of exact gamma_n below Poliakova's bound exists is not in the three papers found; risk that Poliakova's bound is already tight for his family (then we publish a negative table, still a certified number). Compute: shrink delta by bisection per n, each solve 1-120 s, about 10-20 core-h for 40 values of n (cores 1-6). Verification: our two exact checkers (`slr_exact.py`, `check_cover.c`) plus a third Fraction script; Lean `cover` per n costs 30-60 min (do it for 2-3 headline n only). Kill: if after 6 core-h no n improves on the Poliakova rule's gamma by more than 1e-6 relative, stop and report the table as a null. EV: medium (p success ~0.5, value moderate, Tier C, verified numerics).

### 2. SLR-GAP2: more n in 39..94 from a different base (counterexample, new n)

Different from phase 1/2: non-even base (multiples of 3, or odds), larger shift menu (denominators 3, 8, 12 instead of c/4 + j/(8(N+1))), and allowing a free N (the set size n only fixes the count, and N=2^k coincidences like 128=2N, 1/N=2^-6 behind n=63 suggest looking for other N with a nearby power-of-2 or highly composite structure, e.g. N=48, 72, 81, 96 with 3-adic moves). A GF(9)/network-code route (the AIC-MUC9 family from the 30-day plan) is a different object (a linear code, not a cover of R/Z) and I could not see how it transfers; do not count on it. Why new: our phase 1/2 logs show INFEASIBLE or UNKNOWN only within 3/5-smooth pools with c/4 shifts; no paper covers 39..94. Compute: 20-40 core-h, many UNKNOWN outcomes expected. Verification: same checkers, Lean `cover` 30-60 min. Kill: 15 core-h with all pools INFEASIBLE or UNKNOWN. EV: low-medium (p ~0.1-0.15 of a new n, but a new n is a headline; also a second sporadic point would tell whether n=63 is a one-off).
