\begin{abstract}
We turn two standard cosmological statements into theorems checked by the Lean 4 kernel, with the solvers kept outside the
trusted base. For flat $\Lambda$CDM with radiation neglected and the Gaussian DESI DR2 BAO likelihood~\cite{desi}:
(i) the BAO $\chi^2$ exceeds $\Thr$ for every $\Omega_m\in[0,0.255]\cup[0.35,1]$ and every value of the BAO scale
$h r_d$, and is at most $\FitHi$ at a specific point, so these bands are excluded at $\Delta\chi^2>25$ (about $4.6\sigma$ for two parameters);
Einstein--de Sitter has $\chi^2\ge\EdSBound$ at every $h r_d$.
(ii) With the BBN prior $\omega_b=0.02218\pm0.00055$ and the Aubourg et al.\ fitting formula for the sound horizon, the combined $\chi^2$
exceeds $\UTot+25$ for every $\Omega_m\in[0,1]$, every $\omega_b$ and every $h\ge0.73$, while it is at most $\UTot$ at $h=\HStar$:
a certified form of the known tension between BAO+BBN and $H_0\approx73$ (the certified statement is conservative: an independent floating computation puts the $\Delta\chi^2=25$ crossing at $H_0\approx71.6$ and $\Delta\chi^2\approx54.6$ at $H_0=73$). The proofs combine a rational interval evaluator with a soundness
theorem, monotonicity in the parameters, certified bounds of $\log$, $\exp$ and real powers from Mathlib's series estimates, and
integer certificates closed by \texttt{decide +kernel}; only the axioms \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} are used.
The preregistered slab layout of (i) failed, and a disclosed post-hoc refinement was needed. These are certified versions of
results that are already known numerically; there is no new physics here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why certify a likelihood statement}
Statements such as ``$\Omega_m>0.35$ is excluded'' or ``BAO+BBN disfavour $H_0=73$'' are normally established by floating-point
minimisation over nuisance parameters. A kernel-checked version removes the integrator, the minimiser and the floating-point arithmetic from
the trusted base: if the theorem compiles, it holds for the stated model and data, for \emph{every} value of the profiled parameters, not only
those the minimiser visited. This continues a pilot~\cite{p1} that certified BAO distances only. The model and the likelihood remain modelling inputs
that a reader must accept or not; the theorems certify the computation, not the cosmology.

\section{Model, data and statements}
$E(z)=\sqrt{\Omega_m(1+z)^3+1-\Omega_m}$ (flat $\Lambda$CDM, radiation off), $\chi(z)=\int_0^z dz'/E$. The DESI DR2 BAO data vector
(13 points at $z=0.295,\dots,2.33$) and its block-diagonal covariance $C$ are read as exact rationals; $P=C^{-1}$ is computed exactly and the identity
$PC=I$ over the full $13\times13$ matrices is checked by the kernel. Predictions are $K\chi$, $K/E$ and $K(z\chi^2/E)^{1/3}$ for $D_M/r_d$, $D_H/r_d$, $D_V/r_d$,
with $K=c/(100\,h r_d)$. For (ii), $r_d=55.154\,e^{-72.3(\omega_\nu+0.0006)^2}/(\omega_{cb}^{0.25351}\omega_b^{0.12807})$\,Mpc~\cite{aubourg}, $\omega_\nu=0.0107\times0.06$,
$\omega_{cb}=\Omega_m h^2-\omega_\nu$, exactly as in an earlier floating-point analysis whose Lean skeleton of these definitions (monotonicity of $r_d$ and of $h r_d$)
we import unchanged. Table~\ref{tab:res} lists every preregistered statement and its verdict.

\section{Method}
\paragraph{Distances.} As in~\cite{p1}: $1/E$ is decreasing, so Riemann sums on a grid bound $\chi$; an untrusted program supplies integer bounds of $1/E$ at the grid
nodes, which the kernel re-checks exactly ($4661$ nodes per parameter value, $46\,601$ for the fit point).

\paragraph{Likelihood.} $\chi^2$ is a quadratic form in the predictions. Writing $K=K_0+t$ for an untrusted centre $K_0$,
$\chi^2=c'-tB'+t^2A$ with $c'$, $B'$, $A$ polynomials in the base quantities; a rational interval evaluator, proved sound once, bounds them over a box.
Then $A>0$ gives $\chi^2\ge c'-B'^2/(4A)$ for \emph{every} real $K$, which profiles over $h r_d$ exactly. Centring keeps $c'$ and $B'$ small (an uncentred
expansion loses $\sim10^2$ to cancellation and makes the bounds vacuous).

\paragraph{Parameter slabs.} $\chi$, $1/E$ and $(z\chi^2/E)^{1/3}$ decrease in $\Omega_m$, so tables at the two edges of a slab bound every prediction inside it.

\paragraph{The $H_0$ statement.} For $h\ge0.73$, $h r_d$ increases with $h$ (imported theorem), and $r_d$ decreases with $\omega_{cb}$ and $\omega_b$; hence over a box
$\Omega_m\in[a,b]$, $\omega_b\in[c,d]$ every admissible $K$ satisfies $K\le K_0:=c/(100\cdot0.73\,r_d(\omega_{cb}(0.73,b),d))$. Centring at $K_0$, if $B'\ge0$
on the box then $\chi^2(K)\ge c'$ for all $K\le K_0$. The value $r_d$ at the box corner is enclosed by $\exp(c_0-a\log\omega_{cb}-b\log\omega_b)$ with $\log$ bounded by
Mathlib's series for $\log(1-u)$ after scaling by $2^k$, $\log2$ by Mathlib's $10^{-10}$ bracket, and $\exp$ by Mathlib's Taylor bound; the certified bracket at one
point is $[\RdLo,\RdHi]$ (relative width $2.3\times10^{-10}$) and contains the Python value $\RdPy$, about $1.1\times10^{-10}$ (relative) from each end. Outside $\omega_b\in\mu\pm7\sigma$ (including $\omega_b\le0$, where $r_d$ is not physical) the prior term alone exceeds $49$, using a kernel-checked proof that the
BAO $\chi^2$ is non-negative (block positive semidefiniteness of $P$). Outside $\Omega_m\in[0.255,0.35]$ the all-$K$ slab theorems of (i) apply.
The final theorem is assembled by nested case splits that replay the bisection of the $(\Omega_m,\omega_b)$ band into $\NBoxes$ boxes.

\section{Results}
\paragraph{(i) Matter density.} The interval $[\FitLo,\FitHi]$ for $\chi^2$ at the fit point contains the floating values from the rusty-SUNDIALS BAO crate (through its MCP
server, $\MCPFit$) and an independent 40-digit computation. Einstein--de Sitter is excluded at every $h r_d$. The preregistered slab layout excluded only $\PreExcl$ of $\PreN$ slabs: inside a wide slab the
predictions vary so much that the box bound is slack. A refinement by bisection, written after an untrusted preview and disclosed, gives $\RefN$ slab theorems with bounds $\ge\RefMin$,
covering $[0,0.255]\cup[0.35,1]$; the floating $\Delta\chi^2=25$ edges are $0.2577$ and $0.3443$, so the certified band is conservative.
Controls: a copy of the data with one value shifted by $10\sigma$ moves the certified interval to $[\TamperLo,\TamperHi]$; a slab containing the fit point is not excluded;
false bounds fail to compile; planted \texttt{sorry} and axioms are flagged ($\NAudP2$ theorems audited).

\paragraph{(ii) Hubble constant.} $\chi^2_{\rm tot}\le\UTot$ at $(\Omega_m,h,\omega_b)=(0.29743,\HStar,0.02218)$ and $\chi^2_{\rm tot}>\UTot+25$ for all $h\ge0.73$; the weakest box clears the threshold at $\BoxMin$.
With one parameter of interest, $\Delta\chi^2=25$ corresponds to $5\sigma$. Negative controls at the level of the untrusted planner (Python mirror, not Lean): the same pipeline produces no certificate for $h\ge0.686$ (inside the allowed
region) nor for $h\ge0.73$ with the BBN mean shifted to $0.0282$, a value that makes $h=0.73$ acceptable; independent numerics confirm that both target statements are false.
An independent verifier rebuilt all 173 modules of the final theorem's import closure from source and found, among others, that a false threshold of 65 is rejected by the kernel
(a point with $\chi^2_{\rm tot}=64.91$ exists at $h=0.73$), as is a box with its bound raised or its $K_0$ lowered. The lower side ($h\le0.64$) was preregistered as secondary and not attempted.

\section{Limitations and scope}
Radiation is neglected in $E(z)$ (it changes $E(2.33)$ by a few $\times10^{-4}$), while the Aubourg fitting formula was calibrated on Boltzmann-code results that include radiation;
the sound horizon is a fitting formula, not a Boltzmann code; $\Omega_m$ is restricted to $[0,1]$; neutrino density is fixed; the likelihoods are Gaussian and the data
are taken as published; $\Delta\chi^2$ thresholds are conventions. The certified regions are conservative and depend on the box layout; $H_0=73$ is a reference value, not the edge of the excluded region. Nothing here bears on the origin of the tension.
