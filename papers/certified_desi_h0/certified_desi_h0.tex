\begin{abstract}
We turn two standard cosmological statements into theorems checked by the Lean 4 kernel, with solvers and minimisers kept outside the
trusted base. For flat $\Lambda$CDM with radiation neglected and the Gaussian DESI DR2 BAO likelihood~\cite{desi}:
(i) the BAO $\chi^2$ exceeds $\Thr$ for every $\Omega_m\in[0,0.255]\cup[0.35,1]$ and every value of the BAO scale $h r_d$, while it is at most $\FitHi$ at a
specific point, so these bands are excluded at $\Delta\chi^2>25$ with $h r_d$ profiled ($5\sigma$ for one parameter of interest under Wilks' theorem; about $4.6\sigma$
if read as a joint two-parameter region); Einstein--de Sitter has $\chi^2\ge\EdSBound$ at every $h r_d$.
(ii) With the BBN prior $\omega_b=0.02218\pm0.00055$ (DESI DR2 eq.~14~\cite{desi,bbn}) and the Aubourg et al.\ fitting formula for the sound horizon~\cite{aubourg},
the combined $\chi^2$ exceeds $\UTot+25$ for every $\Omega_m\in[0,1]$, every real $\omega_b$ and every $h\ge0.73$, while it is at most $\UTot$ at $h\approx\HStar$:
a certified, conservative form of the known tension between BAO+BBN and the distance-ladder value $H_0\approx73$~\cite{shoes}.
The proofs use a rational interval evaluator with a soundness theorem, monotonicity in the parameters, bounds of $\log$, $\exp$ and real powers
derived from Mathlib's series estimates, and integer certificates closed by \texttt{decide +kernel}; only the axioms \texttt{propext},
\texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound} are used. The preregistered slab layout of (i) failed and a disclosed post-hoc refinement was needed.
These are certified versions of results already known numerically; there is no new physics here.
\end{abstract}

\section{Why certify a likelihood statement}
Statements such as ``$\Omega_m>0.35$ is excluded'' or ``BAO+BBN disfavour $H_0=73$'' are normally established by floating-point minimisation over
nuisance parameters. A kernel-checked version removes the integrator, the minimiser and the floating-point arithmetic from the trusted base: if the
theorem compiles, it holds for the stated model and data for \emph{every} value of the profiled parameters, not only those a minimiser visited. This
continues a pilot that certified BAO distances only~\cite{p1}. The model and the likelihood remain inputs that a reader must accept or not; the theorems
certify the computation, not the cosmology.

\section{Model, data and statements}
$E(z)=\sqrt{\Omega_m(1+z)^3+1-\Omega_m}$ (flat $\Lambda$CDM, radiation off), $\chi(z)=\int_0^z dz'/E$. The DESI DR2 BAO data vector
(13 measurements at 7 effective redshifts, $z=0.295,\dots,2.33$) and its block-diagonal covariance $C$ are read as exact rationals; $P=C^{-1}$ is computed exactly and the
identity $PC=I$ over the full $13\times13$ matrices is checked by the kernel. Predictions are $K\chi$, $K/E$ and $K(z\chi^2/E)^{1/3}$ for $D_M/r_d$, $D_H/r_d$, $D_V/r_d$,
with $K=c/(100\,h r_d)$. For (ii), $r_d=55.154\,e^{-72.3(\omega_\nu+0.0006)^2}/(\omega_{cb}^{\alpha}\omega_b^{\beta})$\,Mpc with $\alpha=0.25351$, $\beta=0.12807$~\cite{aubourg},
$\omega_\nu=0.0107\times0.06$ and $\omega_{cb}=\Omega_m h^2-\omega_\nu$, exactly as in an earlier floating-point analysis by the author (repository \texttt{AutoevolveAI},
\path{results/bao_bbn_h0}, with radiation included, which found $H_0=68.57\pm0.60$); we import its Lean skeleton (\path{formal/ANSE/BAO_BBN_H0.lean},
monotonicity of $r_d$ and of $h r_d$) byte for byte. For comparison, DESI's own BAO+BBN analysis gives $H_0=68.51\pm0.58$~\cite{desi}. Table~\ref{tab:res} lists every
preregistered statement and its verdict.

\section{Method}
\paragraph{Distances.} As in~\cite{p1}: $1/E$ is decreasing, so Riemann sums on a grid bound $\chi$; an untrusted program supplies integer bounds of $1/E$ at the grid
nodes, which the kernel re-checks exactly ($4661$ nodes per parameter value, $46\,601$ for the fit point).

\paragraph{Likelihood.} $\chi^2$ is a quadratic form in the predictions. Writing $K=K_0+t$ for an untrusted centre $K_0$, $\chi^2=c'-tB'+t^2A$ with $c'$, $B'$, $A$ polynomials in
the base quantities; a rational interval evaluator, proved sound once, bounds them over a box. Then $A>0$ gives $\chi^2\ge c'-B'^2/(4A)$ for \emph{every} real $K$, which
profiles over $h r_d$ exactly. Centring (preregistration amendment A1, made before any number of this part was computed) keeps $c'$ and $B'$ small; without it, cancellation
between two terms of size $\sim10^5$ would, by an a priori estimate, cost $\sim10^2$ and make the bounds vacuous.

\paragraph{Parameter slabs.} $\chi$, $1/E$ and $(z\chi^2/E)^{1/3}$ decrease in $\Omega_m$, so tables at the two edges of a slab bound every prediction inside it.

\paragraph{The $H_0$ statement.} On $\{h:\omega_\nu\le(1-2\alpha)\Omega_m h^2\}$, which contains every box below at $h\ge0.73$, $h r_d$ increases with $h$ (imported theorem), and
$r_d$ decreases with $\omega_{cb}$ and $\omega_b$. Hence over a box $\Omega_m\in[\Omega_-,\Omega_+]$, $\omega_b\in[\omega_-,\omega_+]$ every admissible $K$ satisfies
$K\le K_0:=c/(100\cdot0.73\,r_d(\omega_{cb}(0.73,\Omega_+),\omega_+))$. Centring at $K_0$, if $B'\ge0$ on the box then $\chi^2(K)\ge c'$ for all $K\le K_0$. The value of $r_d$ at the
box corner is enclosed through $\exp(c_0-\alpha\log\omega_{cb}-\beta\log\omega_b)$, with $\log$ bounded by Mathlib's series for $\log(1-u)$ after scaling by $2^k$, $\log2$ by
Mathlib's $10^{-10}$ bracket, and $\exp$ by Mathlib's Taylor bound. At $(\omega_{cb},\omega_b)=(0.159228,0.02218)$ the certified bracket is $[\RdLo,\RdHi]$ (relative width
$2.3\times10^{-10}$) and contains the Python value $\RdPy$, within $2.5\times10^{-12}$ of its midpoint. Outside $\omega_b\in\mu\pm7\sigma$ (including $\omega_b\le0$, where $r_d$ is not
physical) the prior term alone exceeds $49$, using a kernel-checked proof that the BAO $\chi^2$ is non-negative (block positive semidefiniteness of $P$). Outside
$\Omega_m\in[0.255,0.35]$ the all-$K$ slab theorems of (i) apply. The final theorem is assembled by nested case splits that replay the bisection of the
$(\Omega_m,\omega_b)$ band into $\NBoxes$ boxes.

\section{Results}
\paragraph{(i) Matter density.} The interval $[\FitLo,\FitHi]$ for $\chi^2$ at the fit point contains the value $\MCPFit$ from the author's own Rust BAO code
(rusty-SUNDIALS, an unmerged branch; not independent of this work's data handling) and a 40-digit value computed in an independent verification run.
Einstein--de Sitter is excluded at every $h r_d$. The preregistered slab layout excluded only $\PreExcl$ of $\PreN$ slabs, so the preregistered prediction
($[0,0.24]\cup[0.36,1]$ excluded) was not met: nine of the eleven failures are slack of the box bound in wide slabs, and the slabs $[0.25,0.26]$ and $[0.34,0.35]$ reach into the
allowed region and cannot be excluded at all. A refinement by bisection, written after an untrusted preview and disclosed as amendment A2 (as the preregistration required for any
change of layout), gives $\RefN$ slab theorems with bounds $\ge\RefMin$ covering $[0,0.255]\cup[0.35,1]$; floating $\Delta\chi^2=25$ edges are $0.2577$ and $0.3443$, so the certified
bands are conservative. Controls: a copy of the data with one value shifted by $10\sigma$ moves the certified interval to $[\TamperLo,\TamperHi]$; a slab containing the fit point is not
excluded; false bounds fail to compile; planted \texttt{sorry} and axioms are flagged (the producer's audit covered $\NAudP2$ theorems; the verification run re-audited a subset).

\paragraph{(ii) Hubble constant.} $\chi^2_{\rm tot}\le\UTot$ at $(\Omega_m,h,\omega_b)=(0.29743,\,h_\star,\,0.02218)$, $h_\star=\HStarExact$; only this upper end was certified, which is all
the exclusion needs (H9 met in that one-sided form). For all $h\ge0.73$, $\chi^2_{\rm tot}>\UTot+25$; the weakest element of the proof is the $\Omega_m$ slab $[0.2525,0.255]$ at $\RefMin$,
the weakest $H_0$ box clears at $\BoxMin$. With one parameter of interest and the others profiled, $\Delta\chi^2=25$ corresponds to $5\sigma$ (about $4.3\sigma$ if read jointly in
$(\Omega_m,h,\omega_b)$). An independent verification run puts the floating $\Delta\chi^2=25$ crossing at $H_0\approx71.6$ and $\Delta\chi^2\approx54.6$ at $H_0=73$; $H_0=73$ is a
reference value, not the edge of the excluded region. That run also rebuilt all 173 modules of the final theorem's import closure from source and found that the proof breaks when the
threshold is raised to 65 (a false statement: $\chi^2_{\rm tot}=64.91$ at $h=0.73$), or when one box's bound is raised or its $K_0$ lowered beyond what its certificate supports (tightness
checks; such a raised bound may itself be true). Planner-level checks (Python mirror, not Lean): for $h\ge0.686$, and for $h\ge0.73$ with the BBN mean moved to $0.0282$, the planner certified
none of its 291 boxes; every failure was the vertex condition $B'\ge0$, at boxes whose $\chi^2$ lower bound was far above threshold, so these runs show that the method does not certify there,
not that it detects a low $\chi^2$; independent numerics show both statements are false. The lower side ($h\le0.64$) was preregistered as secondary and not attempted.

\section{Limitations and scope}
Radiation is neglected in $E(z)$ (it changes $E(2.33)$ by about $5\times10^{-4}$), the massive neutrino is treated as matter at all $z$, while the Aubourg fitting formula was calibrated on
Boltzmann-code results that include radiation (with $N_{\rm eff}=3.046$; DESI uses $3.044$); the sound horizon is a fitting formula; $\Omega_m$ is restricted to $[0,1]$; the likelihoods are
Gaussian and the data are taken as published; $\Delta\chi^2$ thresholds are conventions. The certified regions are conservative and depend on the box layout. Nothing here bears on the origin
of the tension.
