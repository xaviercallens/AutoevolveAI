# Statements in data/ that the paper overrides

`data/FEASIBILITY.md`, `data/result.json` and `data/citation_check.json` are copies of the internal study note's files (results/proposal2/2026-10-26/B3/). They are kept unedited, as shipped results. Where they disagree with `main.tex`, `main.tex` governs. The statements below are overridden (independent audit AUDIT.md, BLOCKING-1 and NB-11).

1. **Rigour claim and scope.** `result.json` field `rigorous_for` and `FEASIBILITY.md` sections 0 and 6 ("rigorous ... for piece-intrinsic ansatz with 4 or fewer parameters") are replaced by: Tier C lemma plus Tier X floating-point (quasi-Monte Carlo, no enclosures) evaluation, for classes invariant under the 60-degree rotation of the hexagonal pyramids (or axial or radial). The number of parameters is not a hypothesis. A piece-intrinsic class with ten parameters is covered; a two-parameter class that reads neighbour data is not. Beyond this scope the negative is only the Tier C counting heuristic.
2. **Tier letter "A".** In the note, "A" means "elementary inequality with a proof sketch". In the paper and ledger, A means kernel-checked, and nothing here is. Every note statement tagged A means C in the paper (or X when it depends on a floating-point number).
3. **Number of configurations.** "33 configurations" (FEASIBILITY.md section 0) should read 32, the number in `code/n2_voronoi.json`.
4. **W_fcc phrases.** "cannot even certify W >= W_fcc" and "nor even exclude energies between bcc and fcc" are withdrawn. Since W_bcc < W_fcc, no valid bound certifies W >= W_fcc. The comparison with the fcc-bcc gap is scale context only: a certificate from these classes lies below W_bcc by at least about that gap and could not exclude a configuration whose energy is below W_bcc by less than that amount.
5. **"D6" labels.** "D6 bound", "D6 group average" and `hex_D6` denote the single-rotation pair bound of Lemma 3(ii) (the 60-degree rotation R, bound (1/2)||(1-R)h||), not a D6 group average.
6. **Cosmetic.** `result.json` `gap_hcp_minus_bcc: 0.00014692` should be 0.00014691 (`code/n1_energies.json`: 1.469092e-4). `gap_fcc_minus_bcc: 8.969e-05` is the rounded value of the computed 8.969202e-5; the paper takes the computed value from `code/n1_energies.json` (the slack script carries a hand-typed 8.969424e-5, which is not used).
7. **Euler transfer.** The paper adds, after the incidence-count transfer, the restriction to simple tessellations (density of generic configurations plus continuity of W_N on separated configurations) and the margin below the separation distance; the note's G4 row already contains the restriction step.
