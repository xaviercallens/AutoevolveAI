\begin{abstract}
For the Gaussian pair potential $V(r)=e^{-\pi r^2}$ and unit-covolume planar Bravais lattices, let $E(t)$ be the harmonic
zero-point energy per particle ($\hbar=m=1$) along the symmetric shear $L_t=P_tL_\rho$, $P_t=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&t\\t&1+t^2\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)$,
starting at the triangular lattice $L_\rho$. We show that $E(t)<E(0)$ for every $0<|t|\le10^{-5}$, so \textbf{the triangular lattice is not
a local minimum of the zero-point energy}. The proof bounds $E$ above by a majorant $U$ that is affine in the dynamical matrix
(a tangent form of the concavity of $\tr\sqrt{\cdot}$) and certifies that the second difference of $U$ is at most
$-1.84156\,t^2$. This is an \emph{upper bound} on a curvature, not a two-sided enclosure of the Laplacian of $\Ezp$ at $\rho$.

\textbf{Tier split.} \tA{}: the Lean~4.34.1 kernel checks the 2$\times$2 concavity inequality, the logic (a majorant window bound with $B<0$ excludes
a local minimum), the rational certificate ($\mathrm{Upp0}_{hi}+\mathrm{trW}_{hi}\,\mathrm{esum}_{hi}<0$, stability margin $>0$) and the assembled
theorem, whose analytic inputs are explicit hypotheses; axioms stay within $\{$\lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound}$\}$,
and planted-\lean{sorry}, planted-axiom, tampered-certificate and $a=2$ controls are caught or rejected.
\tCb{}: the Brillouin-zone integrals, lattice sums and tails are rigorous Arb ball-arithmetic enclosures (206,468 cells, 0 failed) executed by
python-flint, \emph{not kernel-checked}. \tCh{}: the glue ($E\le U$ after integration, the window identity, mirror symmetry, identification with the earlier
$E_{\rm zp}$). Hence the headline is Tier~C overall; only its logic and arithmetic are Tier~A. A corollary $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp(\rho)\le-0.92078$ needs extra hypotheses
($E\in C^2$ and $\Ezp$ of class $C^2$ near $\rho$; $\rho$ critical and the Hessian isotropic, both by the order-3 stabilizer, P2 3.1) and is \tCh{}. The same certificate also works at $a=1/2$ (not preregistered), and fails, as it must, at $a=2$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting, and what was preregistered}\label{sec:setting}
This is a follow-up to the programme paper on the zero-point energy~\cite{p2} (``P2''), which found numerically (Tier~X) that the triangular lattice is \emph{not} the minimiser of
$\Ezp$ for several completely monotone potentials, and that the curvature of $\Ezp$ at $\rho=e^{i\pi/3}$ is negative for Gaussians $e^{-\pi a r^2}$ with $a$ in an interval containing $a=1$
(P2 value $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp(\rho)=-3.848$ at $a=1$, Tier~X). The present work asks for a \emph{certified} statement at $a=1$.

\paragraph{The functional.} Let $L_\rho=c\mathbb Z(1,0)+c\mathbb Z(\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2)$, $c^2=2/\sqrt3$, and $R_n(t)=P_tR_n(0)$ for integer labels $n=(m,l)$. Put
$H_n(t)=\hess V(R_n(t))=e^{-\pi|R|^2}(4\pi^2RR^{\mathsf T}-2\pi I)|_{R=R_n(t)}$ and, at reduced zone coordinate $\kappa\in[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12]^2$,
\[D_t(\kappa)=\sum_{n\ne0}\bigl(1-\cos2\pi\kappa\!\cdot\! n\bigr)H_n(t),\qquad E(t)=\tfrac12\int\tr D_t(\kappa)^{1/2}\,d\kappa .\]
The labels $n$ and $\kappa$ are held fixed as $t$ varies (strain frame, no rotation). $\det P_t=1$, so the covolume stays one, and the lattice shape is $\tau(t)=\rho+\sqrt3\,t+O(t^2)$. \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0006})

\paragraph{Preregistration and deviation.} Item B2 of \texttt{results/proposal2/2026-10-26/preregistration.json} (prior $0.4$) reads: ``the isotropic curvature $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp$ at the triangular point is negative for
$a=1$, certified by interval arithmetic with rigorous tail bounds and checked in Lean; negative control: $a=2$ is not certified negative''; kill rule: ``if the rigorous enclosure cannot exclude 0, report the best certified interval''.
\textbf{What was delivered differs.} A direct enclosure of $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp$ was not attempted; the producer chose the linear-response majorant because it is affine in $D_t$ and needs no matrix square root of the perturbed lattice. (The second variation of $\tr D^{1/2}$ is smooth away from $\Gamma$; a direct certified enclosure of $E''(0)$ with the same Duffy machinery appears feasible and is left open.)
Instead the method certifies a majorant, which yields ``not a local minimum'' (the title) without isotropy or criticality, and a one-sided Laplacian bound only as a \tCh{} corollary with extra hypotheses.
The preregistered literal statement (a two-sided certified $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp<0$, checked in Lean) is therefore \textbf{not} established; what is established is weaker in one respect (one-sided, local, one path) and different in another (no isotropy needed). The negative control ($a=2$ not certified) was run as preregistered.

\section{Step-by-step human demonstration, with the matching Lean statements}\label{sec:human}
Each step states the mathematics, then the Lean counterpart (full text in \texttt{data/lean/Basic.lean}, hash in Section~\ref{sec:lean}). Listings are transliterated to ASCII.

\paragraph{Step 1: a tangent form of the concavity of $\tr\sqrt{\cdot}$ \tA{} (\texttt{CZP-A-0001}).}
Let $X$ be symmetric positive definite, $S$ symmetric, $P=X^{-1}$. Then
\[2\tr S\le\tr X+\tr(PS^2),\qquad\text{with equality at }S=X.\]
\emph{Proof.} Put $M=S-X$. Expanding, $\tr(P(S-X)^2)=\tr(PS^2)-\tr(PSX)-\tr(PXS)+\tr(PX^2)=\tr(PS^2)-2\tr S+\tr X$, using $PX=I$ and $\tr(PSX)=\tr(XPS)=\tr S$. Since $M$ is symmetric, $\tr(PM^2)=\sum_j m_j^{\mathsf T}Pm_j\ge0$ over the columns $m_j$ of $M$, and $P\succ0$. $\square$
In Lean the $2\times2$ case is a \lean{linear\_combination} identity (the displayed trace identity, using the four entries of $XP=I$) plus two uses of \lean{quad\_nonneg} (one per column).
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem quad_nonneg (p11 p12 p22 a b : R) (hp : 0 < p11) (hdet : 0 <= p11*p22 - p12^2) :
    0 <= p11*a^2 + 2*p12*a*b + p22*b^2
theorem two_trace_le_of_inverse (x11 x12 x22 p11 p12 p22 s11 s12 s22 : R) (hp : 0 < p11)
    (hdet : 0 <= p11*p22 - p12^2) (h11 : x11*p11 + x12*p12 = 1) (h12 : x11*p12 + x12*p22 = 0)
    (h21 : x12*p11 + x22*p12 = 0) (h22 : x12*p12 + x22*p22 = 1) :
    2*(s11+s22) <= (x11+x22) + (p11*(s11^2+s12^2) + 2*p12*(s12*(s11+s22)) + p22*(s12^2+s22^2))
\end{lstlisting}
The right side is $\tr X+\tr(PS^2)$ written out for symmetric $S$. The statement is for $2\times2$ matrices only, which is all that is needed (two phonon branches). \lean{two\_trace\_eq\_of\_eq} is the equality case.

\paragraph{Step 2: the majorant $U$ \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0007}).} At each $\kappa\ne0$ take $S=D_t(\kappa)^{1/2}$ (so $S^2=D_t$) and $X=D_0(\kappa)^{1/2}$, positive definite because $L_\rho$ is dynamically stable. Step~1 gives
\[\tr D_t^{1/2}\le\tfrac12\bigl(\tr X+\tr(X^{-1}D_t)\bigr).\]
Integrate: $E\le U$ where $U(t)=\tfrac14\int[\tr X+\tr X^{-1}D_t]\,d\kappa$, and $U(0)=E(0)$ by the equality case. The integrand is integrable because $\tr(X^{-1}D_t)\le\|D_t\|\tr X^{-1}$ and $\int\tr X^{-1}\le1.8336$ (\tCb{}, Step~5).
\emph{Why a majorant:} $U$ is \emph{affine} in $D_t$, so its dependence on $t$ is a single absolutely convergent lattice sum against the fixed matrix $X^{-1}$, with no square root and no branch degeneracy. The price is that $U$ only bounds $E$ from above; the neglected term $\tr(X^{-1}(S-X)^2)\ge0$ can only help the conclusion ``$E$ is smaller''. Lean sees only the pointwise inequality; the integration, integrability and the identification $S=D_t^{1/2}$ are glue. (Because the majorant is built in the strain frame, it is lower-curvature than P2's tau-frame linear response; this frame dependence is expected and is why the number below differs from P2's decomposition.)

\paragraph{Step 3: the window identity \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0008}) with path derivatives \tA{} (\texttt{CZP-A-0006}).}
Since $U$ is affine in $D_t$,
\[U(t)+U(-t)-2U(0)=\tfrac14\int\tr\Bigl(X^{-1}\sum_n(1-\cos2\pi\kappa\!\cdot\! n)\bigl[H_n(t)+H_n(-t)-2H_n(0)\bigr]\Bigr)d\kappa .\]
Entrywise, the second-order mean value theorem gives $H_n(t)+H_n(-t)-2H_n(0)=t^2H_n''(\xi)$ with $|\xi|<t$. Write $H_n''(\xi)=H_n''(0)+(H_n''(\xi)-H_n''(0))$. With $G=\sum_n(1-\cos)H_n''(0)$, $\mathrm{Upp0}=\tfrac14\int\tr(X^{-1}G)\,d\kappa$ $(=U''(0))$, and using $\|W\|_{op}\le\sum|W_{ij}|$ and $1-\cos\le2$ (which, with the prefactors, leaves a slack in the code's constant),
\[\frac{U(t)+U(-t)-2U(0)}{t^2}\le\mathrm{Upp0}+\Bigl(\int\tr X^{-1}\Bigr)\cdot\mathrm{esum},\quad 0<t\le t_0=10^{-5},\]
where $\mathrm{esum}=\sum_{n}\sum_{\rm entries}\sup_{|\xi|\le t_0}|H_n''(\xi)-H_n''(0)|$ is a sum over \emph{all} lattice points (tail included).
The Lean counterpart that \emph{is} kernel-checked is the path calculus: $q(t)=|P_tR_0|^2$, $q'$, $q''$ (\lean{hasDerivAt\_qS}, \lean{hasDerivAt\_q1S}), the Gaussian weight $e=e^{-\pi aq}$ with $e'=-\pi aq'e$ and $e''=e(\pi^2a^2q'^2-\pi aq'')$ (\lean{hasDerivAt\_gaussS}, \lean{hasDerivAt\_gaussS'}, reusing P2's \lean{gauss\_f'}), and the double product rule for one Hessian entry $H=e\,M$:
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem hasDerivAt_entry' {e e1 M M1 : R -> R} {e2 M2 t : R} (he1 : HasDerivAt e1 e2 t)
    (hM : HasDerivAt M (M1 t) t) (he : HasDerivAt e (e1 t) t) (hM1 : HasDerivAt M1 M2 t) :
    HasDerivAt (fun u => e1 u * M u + e u * M1 u) (e2 * M t + 2 * e1 t * M1 t + e t * M2) t
\end{lstlisting}
Assembling these into the entry formula that the code evaluates ($M=4\pi^2a^2R_iR_j-2\pi a\delta_{ij}$ along $P_t$) is \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0009}); a finite-difference check agrees to $7\times10^{-7}$ \tX{}.

\paragraph{Step 4: mirror symmetry \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0010}).} With $\sigma=\mathrm{diag}(1,-1)$: $\sigma L_\rho=L_\rho$ because $\sigma\,c(\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2)=c(1,0)-c(\tfrac12,\tfrac{\sqrt3}2)$, and $\sigma P_t\sigma=P_{-t}$, so $L_{-t}=\sigma L_t$; $E$ depends on the lattice only up to isometry, so $E(-t)=E(t)$. Tier~X check: $E(h)-E(-h)=0.0$ to all printed digits.

\paragraph{Step 5: the numbers \tCb{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0001, -0002}) and their arithmetic \tA{} (\texttt{CZP-A-0003}).} Ball arithmetic encloses $\mathrm{Upp0}=U''(0)$, $\int\tr X^{-1}$, $\mathrm{esum}$ and a stability margin (Section~\ref{sec:numbers}). Their rational upper endpoints are copied into Lean:
\begin{lstlisting}
def Upp0_hi : R := -475041875131 / 250000000000     def trW_hi : R := 366706720019 / 200000000000
def esum_hi : R := 31964165579 / 1000000000000      def t0 : R := 1 / 100000
theorem cert_B_neg : Upp0_hi + trW_hi * esum_hi < 0
theorem cert_stability : 0 < lmin_lo - dB_hi
theorem cert_window (Upp0 trW esum : R) (hU : Upp0 <= Upp0_hi) (hW0 : 0 <= trW) (hW : trW <= trW_hi)
    (he0 : 0 <= esum) (he : esum <= esum_hi) : Upp0 + trW*esum <= Upp0_hi + trW_hi*esum_hi
\end{lstlisting}
The kernel proves the sign and the monotonicity; whether those endpoints really bound the integrals is the \tCb{} content, and that the literals equal the run file is checked by a script (\texttt{lean\_literal\_provenance}, all match). LeanFlow \lean{lean.certify} independently renders $\mathrm{Upp0}_{hi}+\mathrm{pert}_{hi}<0$ and $\mathrm{lmin}_{lo}-\mathrm{dB}_{hi}>0$ into integer statements and verifies both (\tA{}, \texttt{CZP-A-0004}).

\paragraph{Step 6: the logic \tA{} (\texttt{CZP-A-0002}).} Suppose $E\le U$ on $|t|\le t_0$, $U(0)=E(0)$ and $U(t)+U(-t)-2U(0)\le t^2B$ with $B<0$. Then for $0<t\le t_0$: $E(t)+E(-t)\le U(t)+U(-t)\le2U(0)+t^2B<2E(0)$. So at least one of $E(t),E(-t)$ is $<E(0)$ for every small $t$, hence $0$ is not a local minimum; with $E(-t)=E(t)$ (Step~4), both are.
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem not_isLocalMin_of_majorant {E U : R -> R} {t0 B : R} (ht0 : 0 < t0) (hB : B < 0)
    (hEU : forall t, |t| <= t0 -> E t <= U t) (h0 : U 0 = E 0)
    (hU : forall t, 0 < t -> t <= t0 -> U t + U (-t) - 2*U 0 <= t^2 * B) : Not (IsLocalMin E 0)
theorem lt_of_majorant_window_symm ... (hsym : forall t, E (-t) = E t) (ht : 0 < t) (htt : t <= t0) : E t < E 0
\end{lstlisting}

\paragraph{Step 7: assembly \tA{} (conditional, \texttt{CZP-A-0005}) and headline \tier{C} (\texttt{CZP-C-0012}).} \lean{triangular\_not\_localMin\_of\_certificate} takes $E\le U$, $U(0)=E(0)$, the window bound with $\mathrm{Upp0},\mathrm{trW},\mathrm{esum}$, their upper bounds, and $E(-t)=E(t)$ as hypotheses and concludes $\neg\,\mathrm{IsLocalMin}\,E\,0\ \wedge\ \forall t\in(0,t_0],\,E(t)<E(0)$. Every hypothesis is a \tCb{} or \tCh{} fact, so the Lean theorem is a precise statement of what the analytic work must supply, not a proof of the physics. \lean{cert\_stability} is proved but is not a hypothesis of \lean{triangular\_not\_localMin\_of\_certificate}; it supports the \tCh{} discharge of the hypothesis $E\le U$ ($D_t$ must be positive semidefinite for $S=D_t^{1/2}$). The hypothesis $E(-t)=E(t)$ is stated for all $t$ although $|t|\le t_0$ suffices; it is true globally, so nothing depends on this.

\paragraph{Step 8: why this excludes a local minimum of the lattice-shape problem \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0011}).} Because $\tau(t)=\rho+\sqrt3\,t+O(t^2)$, small $t\ne0$ give unit-covolume lattices of shape $\ne\rho$ (mod $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$) arbitrarily close to $\rho$, and each has strictly smaller $\Ezp$ than $L_\rho$. A local minimum of $\Ezp$ over shapes at $\rho$ would require $\Ezp\ge\Ezp(\rho)$ on a shape neighbourhood, contradicted along this path. No criticality or isotropy at $\rho$ is used. (Strict $E(t)<E(0)$ already implies a different shape; the $\tau$-speed computation is needed only for the corollary of Step~9.)

\paragraph{Step 9 (corollary, extra hypotheses) \tCh{} (\texttt{CZP-C-0013}).} If moreover $E$ is $C^2$ at $0$, the window bound gives $E''(0)\le B_{hi}=-1.84156$. If $\Ezp$ is of class $C^2$ near $\rho$ (needed for the chain rule), $\rho$ is critical ($\nabla\Ezp(\rho)=0$, otherwise a $\nabla\Ezp\cdot\tau''(0)$ term appears; this follows from the order-3 stabilizer of $\rho$ in $SL_2(\mathbb Z)$), and the curvature of $\Ezp$ at $\rho$ is isotropic (P2 section~3.1, itself Tier~C; also from the order-3 stabilizer) and $|\tau'(0)|^2/y^2=4$ (so $E''(0)=2\,\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp(\rho)$), then $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp(\rho)\le-0.92078<0$. This is consistent with, and far weaker than, P2's Tier~X value $-3.848$; Tier~X here gives $E''(0)=-7.696$.

As rationals, $\mathrm{Upp0}_{hi}=-475041875131/250000000000$, $\mathrm{trW}_{hi}=366706720019/200000000000$, $\mathrm{esum}_{hi}=31964165579/10^{12}$ (these appear verbatim in the Lean file).
\emph{Meaning.} $B_{hi}$ is an upper bound on a curvature of the majorant $U$, which in turn bounds a second difference of $E$ from above. It is \textbf{not} a two-sided enclosure of $\mathrm{Lap}\,\Ezp(\rho)$ and cannot certify a local minimum; the method bounds $E''\le U''$ from one side only.
Engine details (\tCb{}): the reduced square is split into four triangles with apex $\Gamma$ and Duffy coordinates $\kappa=s\,v(t')$, Jacobian $s/2$; with $D_0=s^2B$ the integrands times the Jacobian are $C^1$ in $(s,t')$ wherever $B>0$, including $s=0$.
Each cell uses the mean-value form $\int_{\rm cell}g\in|{\rm cell}|\,g(\text{centre})+|{\rm cell}|\tfrac h4(\sup|g_s|+\sup|g_{t'}|)[-1,1]$; $64\times64$ cells per triangle, bisected up to depth~3 to $206{,}468$ cells, $0$ failed, $448$~s on one core. The lattice sum is exact over $m^2+ml+l^2\le21$ (42 pairs $\pm n$) and the tails ($|R|\ge5.04$) use the lattice-point count $\#\{|R|\le r\}\le\pi(r+1)^2$ and Gaussian-times-polynomial bounds, below $10^{-21}$ per entry. The remainder beyond the 200 summed shells is bounded in the code by twice the last shell; the decay ratio below $1/2$ behind this is \emph{asserted, not computed}: at $r\approx55$ with shell width $1/4$ and $a\ge1/2$ it is below $e^{-40}$ by the audit's estimate. On coarse grids the certifier fails loudly rather than returning a wrong ball: with $N=16$ cells per triangle side it raises \texttt{fprime\_enc: ball too wide} (the independent audit hit this at $N=16$, depth~2), so it cannot run below roughly $N=32$ without splitting straddling balls. The esum tail contained a factor-2 undercount (\texttt{3*bh2} instead of \texttt{6*bh2}); it is corrected in the code files here, and its effect is nil (tail $2.2\times10^{-25}$ against $9.2\times10^{-13}$ of outward rounding already added to \texttt{esum\_hi}); the certification was \emph{not} re-run, and the reported runs used the earlier file (sha256 below). These bounds are mathematics checked only by being coded; they are not kernel-checked, and a bug in them would not be caught by the Lean theorems.

The $a=2$ run is the method's falsification test: at $a=2$ P2 finds the triangular lattice \emph{is} a local minimum, and the majorant correctly yields a positive bound, so the certificate is not produced. \section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Mostly Tier~C.} Only the logic and the rational arithmetic are kernel-checked. The ball-arithmetic enclosures (the cell bounds, Duffy map, tails, lattice count) are executed code, not proofs; the glue steps (Steps~2--4, 8) are written arguments checked by no machine and no human referee.
\item \textbf{Window and scope.} $|t|\le10^{-5}$, one path, $a=1$, Bravais lattices, harmonic approximation, two dimensions. The theorem is local; it says nothing about the finite competitor $0.5+0.966i$ of P2, which remains Tier~X.
\item \textbf{One-sided.} The method cannot certify a local minimum, and it gives an upper bound on curvature only. The preregistered two-sided certified Laplacian was not obtained (Section~\ref{sec:setting}).
\item \textbf{Statement locked and independently audited by an AI agent (not a human); the audit's corrections are applied; the Tier~A adequacy flags are closed by that audit only to the extent recorded in the ledger's audit fields.} In the ledger the AI audit is recorded in \texttt{ai\_audit}; the gate's \texttt{audit} field stays null, so the eight Tier~A flags remain open. No human has audited the work.
\item \textbf{Repository guards.} \texttt{python antigravity\_guard.py} and \texttt{python test\_rigor\_guard.py} were run at the time of the B2 result and \emph{both fail} for reasons that predate this work: hollow tests in \texttt{tests/test\_k3\_anse\_improvements.py} and \texttt{tests/test\_scientific\_rigor\_v14.py}, and hallucinated-import reports for packages (e.g.\ \texttt{peft}, \texttt{trl}, \texttt{fastmcp}, \texttt{hypothesis}) not installed in the venv. Verbatim outputs: \texttt{data/guard\_antigravity.txt}, \texttt{data/guard\_test\_rigor.txt}. They were not repaired here, and ``guards pass'' is not claimed.
\item \textbf{python-flint location.} python-flint 0.6.0 is not in the project venv; it was installed with \texttt{pip -{}-target} into \texttt{.scratchpad/b2\_pylib} (git-ignored scratch) and put on \texttt{sys.path} by \texttt{b2\_certify.py}. Re-running requires reinstalling that version.
\item \textbf{Missing main-run log.} \texttt{run\_a1\_shear\_main.json} exists, but the plain-text log of the main $a=1$ run was not archived (logs exist for the diagonal, $a=2$ and $a=1/2$ runs). The $b2\_explore2.py$ exploratory output behind the Tier~X cross-checks is likewise not archived; those numbers are quoted from the result note.
\item \textbf{Not novelty-checked.} No literature search was made for prior certified results on zero-point lattice energies; no novelty is claimed.
\end{itemize}
