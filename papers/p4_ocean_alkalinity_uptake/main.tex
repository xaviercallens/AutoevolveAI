\begin{abstract}
For seawater in equilibrium with a fixed fugacity of \COtwo{}, adding one mole of alkalinity (total alkalinity, TA) raises dissolved inorganic carbon (DIC) by
$\eta=\partial\mathrm{DIC}/\partial\mathrm{TA}$ moles. We compute rigorous interval-arithmetic enclosures of $\eta$ over the box
$T\in[0,30]\,^\circ$C, $S\in[30,38]$, $f\mathrm{CO_2}\in[280,560]\,\mu$atm, $\mathrm{TA}=(S/35)\,\mathrm{TA}_{35}$, $\mathrm{TA}_{35}\in[2200,2400]\ \mu$mol\,kg$^{-1}$,
in a carbonate--borate--water model on the total pH scale, with constants as printed in the Dickson--Sabine--Christian Guide~\cite{guide}.
With the constants exactly as printed, $\eta\in[0.7036,\,0.9557]$ over the whole box (Tier~\tC{}, given the constants); the true range contains a value $\ge0.94428$ and a value $\le0.74271$.
An independent AI audit certifies the same interval and a tighter one, $[0.7410,\,0.9455]$. Algebra (the closed form, $0<\eta<1$) is checked in Lean (Tier~\tA{}, statement lock owed).
\textbf{The value $\eta\approx0.8$ and its dependence on temperature, salinity and $p$\COtwo{} are long known}; what is added is an enclosure with stated constants and a monotone closed form.
\textbf{What this is NOT:} no tonnes of \COtwo{} removed, no climate effect, no statement on ecological safety, no deployment advocacy. It is equilibrium thermodynamics of a model: no gas-exchange kinetics, no CaCO$_3$ saturation, no mixing, no organic alkalinity, surface pressure only.
Also reported: a dilute-water (soil) pH scan (Tier~\tX{}), and a partial reproduction of the alkalinity-front retardation arithmetic of Jessen et al., for which the printed numbers are not all mutually consistent.
\end{abstract}

\section{Model, quantity and scope}\label{sec:model}
\paragraph{Model.} Carbonate, borate and water alkalinity on the total pH scale, with $h=[\mathrm H^+]$ (mol\,kg$^{-1}$, total scale):
\[
\mathrm{DIC}=C\Bigl(1+\frac{K_1}{h}+\frac{K_1K_2}{h^2}\Bigr),\qquad
\mathrm{TA}=C\Bigl(\frac{K_1}{h}+\frac{2K_1K_2}{h^2}\Bigr)+\frac{B_TK_B}{K_B+h}+\frac{K_W}{h}-h,\qquad C=K_0\,f\mathrm{CO_2}.
\]
Sulfate, fluoride, phosphate and silicate are set to zero (their effect is quantified in Section~\ref{sec:caveats}). The total scale means $-h$ already stands for $-[\mathrm H^+]_F-[\mathrm{HSO_4^-}]$. Total boron $B_T=0.000416\,S/35$ mol\,kg$^{-1}$. $f\mathrm{CO_2}$ is the independent variable (no fugacity coefficient). The TA definition is the carbonate--borate--water one just written; any other definition of TA (organic alkalinity, nutrient contributions) changes $\eta$ (Section~\ref{sec:caveats}).
\paragraph{Quantity.} At fixed $f\mathrm{CO_2}$ ($C$ fixed), $\eta=(\partial\mathrm{DIC}/\partial h)/(\partial\mathrm{TA}/\partial h)$, which gives
\[
\eta=\frac{C\,(K_1/h^2+2K_1K_2/h^3)}{C\,(K_1/h^2+4K_1K_2/h^3)+B_TK_B/(K_B+h)^2+K_W/h^2+1}
=\frac{h+2K_2}{(h+4K_2)+h^3R/(CK_1)},
\]
$R=B_TK_B/(K_B+h)^2+K_W/h^2+1$. Lean proves that the two forms agree (\lean{eta\_rewrite}) and $0<\eta<1$ for $h,K_1,K_2,C>0$, $R\ge0$. The producer's closed form was checked against a 50-digit numerical derivative of $\mathrm{DIC}(\mathrm{TA})$ at $50$ random points (worst relative difference $1.3\cdot10^{-16}$) and, independently, by the auditor at three points including both extreme corners (agreement $5\cdot10^{-9}$, limited by the auditor's numerical-derivative step, not by the closed form).
\paragraph{Mean-value remark.} A finite alkalinity addition $\Delta\mathrm{TA}$ has an efficiency equal to a mean of $\eta$ over the TA segment; it lies in the enclosure if the whole segment lies in the box.
\paragraph{Scope.} Equilibrium thermodynamics of a model. $\eta$ is a model derivative, not a measurement-and-verification protocol.

\section{Prior art}\label{sec:prior}
To our knowledge $\eta\approx0.8$ at $25\,^\circ$C, and its dependence on temperature, salinity, $p$\COtwo{} and TA, is long known (for instance Zeebe and Wolf-Gladrow~\cite{zwg}; Renforth and Henderson 2017; the OAE efficiency literature), and the closed form is a ratio of buffer derivatives. None of these was re-read for this paper and no systematic literature search was made (neither by the producer nor by the auditor). The only addition attempted is a rigorous enclosure with stated constants and a monotone closed form.

\section{Method}\label{sec:method}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep=1pt
\item $\mathrm{TA}(h)$ is strictly decreasing in $h$, so $h$ is bracketed rigorously (interval sign test of $\mathrm{TA}(h)-\mathrm{TA}$ at both ends, bisection tightening, \texttt{mpmath.iv}, 30 digits). The bracket is split into $24$ pieces. On each piece where an interval test certifies $\mathrm d\eta/\mathrm dh>0$, $\eta$ is evaluated at the two ends only. Lean proves the algebraic identity $N'D-ND'=A\,(2Ah^2K_2+Q)$ for hand-supplied $N'$, $D'$ and $Q\le0$ (\lean{eta\_deriv\_identity}, \lean{Q\_nonpos}); \textbf{that $N'$ and $D'$ are the $h$-derivatives of $N$ and $D$ was checked by hand, by the producer and by the auditor, not in Lean.}
\item Global enclosure by branch-and-bound over $(T,S,f\mathrm{CO_2},\mathrm{TA}_{35})$: the upper bound is the largest live-cell upper bound once every other cell is pruned against a rigorous point lower bound, and symmetrically for the lower bound. A cell is discarded only if its rigorous bound is beaten by a rigorous point bound (soundness re-read by the auditor). Constants per cell are enclosed by hulls over $24\times6$ sub-intervals. \textbf{Both U0 runs stopped at the evaluation cap ($3000$ evaluations, about $1300$ live cells) and not at convergence, so the bounds are sound but not tight.}
\item Constants (Section~\ref{sec:const}): Weiss 1974 ($K_0$), Lueker et al.\ 2000 ($K_1,K_2$), Dickson 1990 ($K_B$), Millero 1995 ($K_W$), Uppstrom 1974 ($B_T$), as printed in the Guide~\cite{guide}.
\item Scenarios: U0, constants exactly as printed; U1 and U2, the pK values of $K_0,K_1,K_2,K_B,K_W$ widened by $\pm1\sigma$ and $\pm2\sigma$ ($\sigma=0.002,0.0075,0.015,0.01,0.01$, the values shipped in PyCO2SYS \texttt{uncertainty.pKs\_OEDG18}, a secondary source). These are input intervals, not probability statements.
\end{enumerate}
The preregistration (\texttt{prereg\_K2.json}, sha256 \texttt{24b99955\dots50000}, verified by the auditor) was, by the producer's statement, written and hashed before any attempt; it was committed together with the results (commit \texttt{e0fbdba}), so git cannot confirm the order.

\paragraph{Reading.} Per mole of alkalinity added, about $0.70$ to $0.96$ mol of DIC is taken up at equilibrium over this box in the model. On grids, $\eta$ is larger when cold, at low salinity-normalised alkalinity and at high $p$\COtwo{} (lower pH): finite differences on a $5^4$ grid have uniform signs ($\eta$ decreases with $T$, $S$, $\mathrm{TA}_{35}$ and increases with $f\mathrm{CO_2}$), and corners plus a $7^4$ interior grid give the range $[0.742708,\,0.944283]$. \textbf{These monotone trends are a numerical observation (Tier~\tX{}), not certified} (correction 10).

\subsection{(b) Dilute and soil water, Tier \tX{}}
Same algebra without borate, with freshwater $K_1$, $K_2$ (Millero 1979 as transcribed in PyCO2SYS; the coefficients $290.9097/-14554.21/-45.0575$ and $207.6548/-11843.79/-33.6485$ were matched by the auditor to PyCO2SYS \texttt{p1atm.py} and give pK$_1=6.351$, pK$_2=10.329$ at $25\,^\circ$C). For $f\mathrm{CO_2}=420\,\mu$atm and $0$--$30\,^\circ$C: $\eta<0.01$ below pH about $4.5$ to $4.7$, $\eta<0.1$ below about $5.0$ to $5.2$, $\eta=0.5$ at about $5.5$ to $5.7$ (outer pH intervals of width $0.13$, \texttt{data/data/soil\_result.json}); at $f\mathrm{CO_2}=10\,000\,\mu$atm every threshold is about $0.7$ pH units lower. Reading: in this regime $\eta\approx[\mathrm{HCO_3^-}]/([\mathrm{HCO_3^-}]+h)$; the auditor's closed-form estimate $h^2=CK_1$ puts $\eta=0.5$ at pH $5.60$ ($25\,^\circ$C) and $5.54$ ($0\,^\circ$C), inside the producer's range. \textbf{Limitations (correction 7):} freshwater $K_1,K_2$ are not pinned to a primary source; the pK uncertainty $\pm0.02$ is an assumption; activity corrections at soil-water ionic strength are ignored; and $K_W$ is the seawater expression evaluated at $S=0$ including the Guide's $-0.015$ total-scale shift, which does not apply in fresh water ($K_W$ about $1.5\%$ low, negligible below pH~$6$).

\subsection{(c) Alkalinity-front retardation (arithmetic of Jessen et al.)}
Jessen et al.~\cite{jessen} (preprint, ``Subsoil acidity causes long delays in inorganic carbon sequestration by Enhanced Weathering'') describe a downward-progressing front of alkalinity delayed by cation-exchange retardation $R=1+\Delta q/\Delta c$. With the delay $T(\mathrm{CEC},L,q)=(L/q)(\varepsilon_w+k\,\mathrm{CEC})$, $k=10\,\Delta\beta(1-\varepsilon)\rho/\Delta c$, equal to $LR/v$ with $v=q/\varepsilon_w$ (Lean: \lean{delay\_via\_R}), the delay is increasing in CEC, $L$, $k$ and decreasing in $q$ (Lean: \lean{delay\_mono\_cec}, \lean{delay\_mono\_L}, \lean{delay\_mono\_k}, \lean{delay\_anti\_q}; Tier~\tA{}, algebra only).
\paragraph{Reproduction (control P4; correction 6).} The printed inputs (as read by the producer and re-read by the auditor from Supplementary Text~2) are $\Delta\beta=(0.55-0.2)+(0.12-0.03)=0.44$, $\varepsilon=0.3$, $\rho=2.65$, $\varepsilon_w=0.1$, $\Delta c=0.80$ meq\,L$^{-1}$, unretarded velocity $5$ m\,yr$^{-1}$, with CEC $0.56$ (sum of exchangers) and $1.6$ (direct). They give $\Delta q=45.71$ and $130.59$ meq\,L$^{-1}$, $R=58.13$ and $164.24$, $v=8.60$ and $3.04$ cm\,yr$^{-1}$ and $11.6$ to $32.8$ yr\,m$^{-1}$. \textbf{$R$ and $v$ pass within printed rounding} (printed $R=58$ and $165$, $v=8.7$ and $3.0$ cm\,yr$^{-1}$). However the printed $\Delta q$ values ($45.4$, $131.3$) have ratio $0.3458$, whereas $\Delta q$ is proportional to CEC, which requires $0.56/1.6=0.35$; the printed $R$ and $v$ follow from the printed $\Delta q$ (for example $1+45.4/0.8=57.75$ gives $8.66$ cm\,yr$^{-1}$). The printed upper value ``34 yr\,m$^{-1}$'' is not reproduced ($32.8$ from the inputs, $33.3$ even from the printed $3.0$ cm\,yr$^{-1}$). \textbf{P4 is therefore a partial pass}, not a pass to the digit as preregistered. This is stated neutrally; the authors were not contacted, and the Jessen PHREEQC model and Dataverse files were not examined.
\paragraph{Table.} Delay in yr\,m$^{-1}$ over CEC and flux is in \texttt{data/data/delay\_result.json} (for instance CEC $[1,2]$, $q\in[0.5,1]$ m\,yr$^{-1}$, $L=1$ m: $[10.3,\,41.0]$). A $\pm10\%$ sensitivity on $\Delta\beta$ and $\Delta c$ is Tier~\tX{}.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item The Guide's five printed check values at $S=35$, $25\,^\circ$C ($\ln K_0=-3.5617$, $\log_{10}K_1=-5.8472$, $\log_{10}K_2=-8.9660$, $\ln K_B=-19.7964$, $\ln K_W=-30.434$) are reproduced by the producer (5 of 5) and by the auditor, who typed the equations himself from the Guide text and obtained $-3.561652$, $-5.8471529$, $-8.9659515$, $-19.796402$, $-30.433844$.
\item The producer's constants agree with PyCO2SYS's for $K_0,K_1,K_2,K_B$ to $10^{-14}$, an independent confirmation of the transcription (not of the Guide's own fidelity to the primary papers). $K_W$ differs by $-1.49\%$ (the Guide subtracts $0.015$ from the constant term to approximate the SWS-to-total conversion; PyCO2SYS converts exactly) and $B_T$ by $+0.07\%$ (the Guide rounds Uppstrom's $0.0004157$ to $0.000416$).
\item \textbf{No second CO2SYS-type package} (mocsy, seacarb) is available. The auditor's independent implementation is his own 50-digit \texttt{model.py} (constants typed from the Guide, own root solver and closed form). His PyCO2SYS cross-check at $202$ points (seed $777$, including both extreme corners): worst relative difference $4.4\cdot10^{-5}$ with sulfate $=$ fluoride $=0$, and $1.4\cdot10^{-10}$ when PyCO2SYS is fed the Guide's $K_W$ and $B_T$.

\end{itemize}

\section{Omitted terms and caveats (correction 4)}\label{sec:caveats}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Sulfate and fluoride} omitted: $\eta$ changes by $+2\cdot10^{-6}$ to $+7.2\cdot10^{-5}$ over $202$ sampled points (Tier~\tX{}, PyCO2SYS, auditor; the producer's ``at most $6.8\cdot10^{-5}$'' was a $100$-sample maximum). With total-scale $h$ the $\mathrm{HSO_4^-}$ term is already contained in $-h$; the real omissions are HF and the $K_B$/$K_W$ scale conversions.
\item \textbf{Phosphate $2$ and silicate $100\ \mu$mol\,kg$^{-1}$} lower $\eta$ by $0.0004$ to $0.0015$ (up to $0.0022$ at $3/150$ with sulfate and fluoride). Even then the result stays inside the U0 enclosure (minimum $0.7427-0.0022=0.7405>0.7036$).
\item \textbf{Boron.} The Lee et al.\ 2010 alternative ($B_T=0.0004326\,S/35$, about $+4\%$) lowers $\eta$ by $0.0004$ to $0.0007$ at the extreme corners ($-7.2\cdot10^{-4}$ and $-4.3\cdot10^{-4}$); Uppstrom's unrounded $0.0004157$ changes it by $+1.3\cdot10^{-5}$ and $+7.8\cdot10^{-6}$; the $K_W$ shift by $-4.6\cdot10^{-5}$ and $-2.1\cdot10^{-6}$.
\item \textbf{Not bounded:} organic alkalinity (unbounded in principle for coastal waters) and pressure (surface, $1$ atm only). Fugacity coefficient: none, because $f\mathrm{CO_2}$ is the input.
\item The claim is \textbf{model-conditional on the total pH scale and on the TA definition stated in Section~\ref{sec:model}.}
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations and what remains unverified}\label{sec:lim}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Model only; no real-world claim.} No tonnes, climate effect, ecological safety or deployment advice. Equilibrium thermodynamics: no gas-exchange kinetics, no CaCO$_3$ precipitation or saturation state, no mixing.
\item \textbf{Constants not verified against primary papers}; they rest on the Guide transcription plus PyCO2SYS (Section~\ref{sec:const}). The kill criterion of the preregistration (constants not pinned to primary sources $\Rightarrow$ Tier~\tX{}) was applied per part: (a) is reported as Tier~\tC{} \emph{with this caveat}, (b) as Tier~\tX{}.
\item \textbf{AI audit only; human audit and Lean statement lock owed.} No second CO2SYS package; interval library not independent.
\item \textbf{U0 is loose} (branch-and-bound stopped at the cap); \textbf{U1/U2 are loose} (pK intervals not subdivided); no tight certified U1/U2.
\item \textbf{Monotone trends} and the sharp-side corner values are Tier~\tX{}/point bounds.
\item \textbf{Literature novelty} not searched; \textbf{Jessen} PHREEQC model and Dataverse files not examined (licence not verified).
\item \textbf{Deviations from the preregistration.} P4 tolerance relaxed to printed rounding (and then found a partial pass); P1 operationalised as a neighbourhood box and P1b added after seeing the $4\cdot10^{-5}$ offset; the time box ($\approx4$ agent-hours) was exceeded because the branch-and-bound was redesigned twice (the first grid enclosures were far too loose, $\eta\in[0.69,0.96]$ with $12\%$ TA spread per cell); the \texttt{grid} entry in \texttt{seawater\_result.json} is a leftover of the abandoned design and is not used.
\item \textbf{Outcome.} PARTIAL: (a) certified given the pinned constants, (b) Tier~\tX{}, (c) algebra in Lean and arithmetic reproduced with an inconsistency in the source.
\end{itemize}
