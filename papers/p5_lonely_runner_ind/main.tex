\begin{abstract}
Sungkawichai and Trakulthongchai (S--T) reduce the lonely runner conjecture for $11$ runners ($k=10$ moving speeds plus the stationary runner) to a finite
computation: for each of $61$ primes $p\in P_{10}=\{127,\dots,467\}$ a set $J(10,p)$ must be empty~\cite{st}. We re-ran that computation
with \emph{independently written} code. The implementer did not read the S--T repository; the independent auditor opened their logs only after its own counts were complete.
\textbf{We find $J(10,p)=\emptyset$ for all $61$ primes of $P_{10}$} (exact integer arithmetic, Tier~\tC{}). The step that S--T discharge with their Proposition~1.4 is
replaced here by a direct computation: for every $p$ we checked all $2\,593\,742\,461$ level-$11$ vectors (up to scaling) over the class of $(1,\dots,10)$ and found $0$ improper ones.
The auditor confirmed this fibre check independently at $p=127,137,263,467$, and checked S--T Proposition~4.1 exhaustively ($15\,937\,424\,600$ vectors) and the Lemma~4.3 inclusion at all $61$ primes.
The scalar inputs are kernel-checked in Lean (Tier~\tA{}): $P_{10}$ is $61$ distinct primes, all $>110$, equal to row $k=10$ of S--T's Table~1, and $55^{90}\le10^{10}\prod P_{10}$, with a margin of $5.08$ nats that disappears without the prime $467$.
\textbf{This is a replication, not a new theorem.} Together with the literature premises $\LRC{9}$, the Malikiosis--Santos--Schymura/Rosenfeld bound and S--T's Corollary~2.5 and Proposition~2.7 (Tier~\tL{}), the computation gives $\LRC{10}$, the conjecture for $11$ runners, which is S--T's Theorem~1.3 for $k=10$. Nothing here is new beyond, to our knowledge, being the first reimplementation of the $k=10$ computation not derived from the S--T code. The lonely runner conjecture in general is open.

\medskip
\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\linewidth}{\small\textbf{What this is NOT.} It is \textbf{not} a proof of anything new, \textbf{not} a proof of the lonely runner conjecture, and \textbf{not} a formalisation of the computation: only scalar inputs are in Lean. The chain to $\LRC{10}$ rests on literature premises we did not re-derive (Section~\ref{sec:tiers}). ``Independent'' means independent \emph{code}; the mathematical reduction is S--T's.}}
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting and question}\label{sec:setting}
\paragraph{Conjecture and notation.} $\LRC{k}$ denotes the lonely runner conjecture for $k+1$ runners: $k$ runners with distinct positive integer speeds and one stationary runner, on a circle of circumference $1$; for every choice of speeds some time exists at which every moving runner is at distance at least $1/(k+1)$ from the stationary one. So $\LRC{9}$ is the case of $10$ runners and $\LRC{10}$ the case of $11$ runners. Any text calling the present result ``ten runners'' would be wrong.

\paragraph{The S--T reduction (Tier \tL{}, as read by the producer and re-read by the auditor).} Write $\|x\|$ for the distance of $x$ to the nearest integer. For a prime $p$ and $l\ge1$ let $N=lp$ and let $u\in(\Z_N)^k$ have no coordinate $\equiv0\pmod p$.
By S--T Definitions 2.1 and 2.3, $u$ is \emph{$(k,p,l)$-proper} if (gcd) for some index $i$, $\gcd(l,u_j:j\ne i)>1$, or (time) some $t\in\tfrac1{lp}\Z$ has $\|tu_i\|\ge\tfrac1{k+1}$ for all $i$. Let $I(k,p,l)$ be the set of improper tuples. $J(k,p)$ is the set of $v\in\Z_p^{*k}$ lying in the reduction mod $p$ of $I(k,p,l)$ for \emph{every} $l$. We implement properness exactly as: (gcd) for some prime $q\mid l$ at most one $u_j$ is not divisible by $q$; or (time) some $a\in[1,N/2]$ has $(k+1)\min(r,N-r)\ge N$ for every $r=au_i\bmod N$. This is the same condition in integers and uses no floating point.
The results of S--T used (Tier \tL{}; \texttt{LRI-L-0002}): Lemma~2.4 ($J=\emptyset$ iff $I(k,p,l)=\emptyset$ for some $l$); Corollary~2.5 (if $\LRC{k-1}$ holds and $J(k,p)=\emptyset$ then $p$ divides $u_1\cdots u_k$ for every counterexample $u$); Lemma~2.6 (the Malikiosis--Santos--Schymura bound in the form of Rosenfeld's Corollary~3: a counterexample has $\prod u_i<B_k$ with $B_k=\bigl(\binom{k+1}{2}^{k-1}/k\bigr)^k$, with division by $k$, not $k!$); Proposition~2.7 (if $\LRC{k-1}$ holds, $J(k,p)=\emptyset$ for all $p$ in a set $P$ and $\prod P\ge B_k$, then $\LRC{k}$ holds); and for $k=10$ the plan $\times2,\times2,\times2,\div8$ of their Section~5.2.

\paragraph{The question.} The preregistration (\texttt{data/preregistration.json}, sha256 \texttt{140a28ad\dots}, item LR-IND, class (d): an independent re-run) asked: re-derive $J(10,p)=\emptyset$ for each $p\in P_{10}$ with code written without reading the S--T repository, run positive and negative controls first, and do not call the result a new proof or claim novelty for $\LRC{10}$. The answer is yes for all $61$ primes. The preregistered text of the item cites Allikvere for the claim that the full computation was not independently reimplemented; that attribution is wrong (Section~\ref{sec:corr}, correction~1) and is not used here as evidence.

\section{Method}\label{sec:method}
Producer code is in \texttt{data/code/} (C99 \texttt{lr\_sieve.c}, \texttt{fiber\_check.c}; Python drivers and checkers; the C sources are also stored as \texttt{.c.txt} so that the bundler, which keeps only listed suffixes, includes them). The frozen binaries' sha256 are in \texttt{data/logs/freeze\_sha256.txt}; compiled binaries are not shipped.
The producer wrote the code from the definitions of arXiv 2604.23906 v2 (Defs.~2.1, 2.3, Prop.~3.1, \S\S5.1--5.2) alone; \texttt{data/result.json} records the sha256 of every file before any reading of the S--T or Trakulthongchai repositories. The auditor could not verify that attestation (only the producer can attest it); qualitatively the code is unlike the S--T code (single-file C99 with bitsets, against C++23 templates) and the auditor saw no sign of copying.

\paragraph{Phase 1: classes of $I(k,p,1)$.}\tC{} One representative per class under permutation, sign and multiplication by $\Z_p^*$. A hitting-set branch: pick a surviving time with the fewest available killers, branch on which speed kills it (excluding siblings already tried), and prune with a capacity bound (the $r$ largest remaining kill counts must cover the surviving times). Supports smaller than $k$ are expanded to all multiplicities at the leaf where they become improper. The branching idea is Rosenfeld's \cite{ros}; the two pruning steps were added after a projection of about $230$ core-hours for $p=467$ alone with the plain search (a kill criterion of $40$ core-hours was preregistered).

\paragraph{Our own pruning (not from the literature): set-canonical supports.}\tC{} $U=\{1=u_1<u_2<\cdots\}\subseteq[1,(p-1)/2]$ is \emph{set-canonical} if it is lexicographically $\le\mathrm{fold}(x^{-1}U)$ for every $x\in U$, where $\mathrm{fold}(v)=\min(v,p-v)$. Soundness (re-derived by the auditor): every class has a set-canonical member, since $\mathrm{fold}$ merges no two distinct elements of $[1,(p-1)/2]$ that are not $\pm$ each other, so one takes the lexicographically least normalisation; and for distinct $x,y\in U$, $\mathrm{fold}(y/x)\ge u_2$, because the second smallest element of $\mathrm{fold}(x^{-1}U)$ must be $\ge u_2$. Multiplicities travel with the support, so emitting all multiplicity patterns of a set-canonical support is complete. The capacity bound is a valid relaxation.

\paragraph{Lifting and projection.}\tC{} By S--T Proposition~3.1 all $c^k$ preimages of each retained tuple are enumerated and the $(k,p,cl)$-improper ones are kept; projection reduces mod $p$ and canonicalises. For $k=10$ the plan is $2,2,2,/$ (three lifts by $2$, then projection). The soundness of this chain is the invariant of Section~\ref{sec:corr} (correction~3), not an argument about representatives alone. Each level reports the number of distinct classes of $\pi_p(S)$ and a hash of them; all cross-checks compare at this level, which does not depend on the representatives used.

\paragraph{The level-11 fibre (replacing Proposition~1.4).}\tC{} After the plan $2,2,2,/$ the only surviving class is that of $(1,\dots,10)$ at every $p\in P_{10}$ (the \emph{identity class}), as S--T's Remark~3.2 predicts. S--T discharge this class by their Proposition~1.4 ($k+1=11$ and $p>k(k+1)$ prime). We instead computed: \texttt{fiber\_check 10 p 11} enumerates all $w\in(\Z_{11})^{10}$ up to $\Z_{11}^*$ scaling (the zero vector and the vectors whose first nonzero coordinate is $1$: $1+(11^{10}-1)/10=2\,593\,742\,461$), forms $u$ by the Chinese remainder theorem with $u\equiv(1,\dots,10)\pmod p$ and $u\equiv w\pmod{11}$, and tests $(10,p,11)$-properness exactly. Scaling $w\mapsto cw$ ($c\in\Z_{11}^*$) with the matching time is a bijection preserving both tests (audited sound). Total over the $61$ primes: $158\,218\,290\,121$ fibre vectors, $0$ improper.
A second program, the generic $\times11$ lift in \texttt{lr\_sieve}, enumerated all $25\,937\,424\,601$ lifts at $p=127$ and left $0$ (a pre-freeze binary built from the same source as the frozen one).

\paragraph{Optional premise re-run.}\tC{} $\LRC{9}$: \texttt{lr\_sieve 9 p 2,5}, i.e.\ $I(9,p,10)$, is empty for all $47$ primes of Trakulthongchai's $S_9$ ($137..401$), $214$ core-seconds. This checks his computation; it is not used as a derivation and $\LRC{9}$ remains a literature premise.

\section{Audit corrections applied}\label{sec:corr}
The independent audit (\texttt{AUDIT.md}, \S6) required six corrections to the producer's report. They are applied in this paper as follows.
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Attribution.} The producer's report quoted Allikvere (arXiv 2609.02604 v2) as saying that ``the full computation has not been independently reimplemented or formally verified''. That sentence in Allikvere \S6 refers to \emph{his own} $k=13/14$ computation, not to S--T's $k=10$. It is not used as evidence. The only novelty statement kept is: to our knowledge (a web and alphaXiv search on 2026-10-28; recency-ranked) this is the first reimplementation of the S--T $k=10$ computation not derived from the S--T code. Allikvere re-inspected S--T's $k=12$ residual tuples, not $k=10$. The searches found no later reimplementation; the newest related items (for example arXiv 2610.23790 on a gap bound, and 2609.06259) are not reimplementations.
\item \textbf{Control column and $k=4$, $p=13$.} Renamed and withdrawn as in Section~\ref{sec:controls}; \texttt{controls\_ok} is true.
\item \textbf{The soundness of lifting.} The producer's report argued that lifting only representatives is sound, via the identity $\pi_p\bigl(\pi^{-1}S\cap I(k,p,cl)\bigr)=\pi_pS\cap\pi_pI(k,p,cl)$. That identity is false for a general $S$ (it holds when $S$ contains every level-$l$ improper tuple over $\pi_pS$); the same identity appears in the proof of S--T Proposition~3.1. We replace it by the invariant actually used. Starting from level-$1$ representatives $r$ and lifting \emph{full fibres}, the retained set at level $l$ is exactly
$$F_l(r)=\{w\in I(k,p,l):\ w\equiv r\pmod p\},$$
by Trakulthongchai's Lemma~7 \cite{trak8} (a tuple improper at $cl$ is improper at $l$ after reduction), as also stated in Allikvere \S2 \cite{alli} (both read by the auditor, not re-proved here). Hence $r\in J$ implies $F_l(r)\ne\emptyset$ at every level, and $J\subseteq\{$classes of $\pi_p(S_n)\}$ at every step. The computation is unaffected, because every plan used chains full fibres from level~$1$.
\item \textbf{Negative controls.} Stated in Section~\ref{sec:controls}: both planted mutations are inert at the level-$11$ step; \texttt{fiber\_check.c} was never mutated; the strict-inequality boundary control and the agreement of the nonzero orbit counts ($54\,976$ at $p=13$; $7\,210$ at $p=23$) are cited.
\item \textbf{Wording of the Tier \tL{} consequence.} Stated in Section~\ref{sec:tiers}: the chain gives $\LRC{10}$, the conjecture for $11$ runners, via the premises $\LRC9$ ($10$ runners), Lemma~2.6 and S--T Corollary~2.5 / Proposition~2.7; it recomputes S--T's Theorem~1.3 for $k=10$ and is not a new result.
\item \textbf{Python helpers.} They ran under system \texttt{python3} without \texttt{-I}, reading only files we wrote; harmless. They should be rerun with \texttt{-I} if regenerated. (The ledger scripts of this paper run with \texttt{-I}.)
\end{enumerate}
\paragraph{An observation on the exposition (not an accusation).} The auditor gives a counterexample to the displayed identity in correction~3: take $k=10$, $p=127$, $v=(1,\dots,10)$ and $S=\{s\}$ where $s$ is the level-$2$ lift with every coordinate even ($s_i=i$ or $i+127$). Then $s$ is gcd-proper, so every level-$4$ lift of $s$ is gcd-proper, hence $S'=\emptyset$ and the left side is empty; but $v$ is improper at level $4$ (no time witness since $11\nmid4p$; five odd coordinates, so the gcd test fails), so $v\in\pi_pI(10,127,4)$ and the right side is $\{v\}$. Here $v\notin J$, so this refutes a proof \emph{step} as displayed, not the proposition's conclusion, and not S--T's computation. This is recorded as an observation about the exposition (also in the proof of S--T Proposition~3.1) and nothing more. It has not been raised with the authors; if anyone raises it, it should go to the authors first, after a second check.

\section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Replication, not a new theorem.} The result recomputes S--T's computational input for $k=10$; $\LRC{10}$ (and Theorem~1.3 of S--T for $k=10$) is theirs. Novelty is only ``to our knowledge, first reimplementation not derived from the S--T code'' (a literature and web search of one day, not a priority claim). The lonely runner conjecture in general is open.
\item \textbf{AI audit only; human audit and statement lock owed.} No person has certified that the Lean statements mean what their gloss says, and no statement lock exists.
\item \textbf{Unverified, from the audit.} (a) Literature premises (Tier \tL{}) not re-derived: $\LRC9$ (EJC 33(2) P2.46) and whether the producer's $k=9$ re-run matches his $S_9$ (not audited); the Malikiosis--Santos--Schymura bound and Rosenfeld's Corollary~3 (the AM--GM step was read); the ``pre-jump'' Lemma~2.2; Lemma~2.4 (read and accepted). (b) \textbf{Phase 1 and lifts at $58$ of the $61$ primes, including $p=467$,} were not re-run with the auditor's own algorithm (own-algorithm coverage: $127,137,263$); they rest on the producer's code (rebuilt bit-identically), its mode agreements and exact $S_1$ agreement with the S--T log at all $61$ primes. (c) The \textbf{unpruned} producer pass was finished at $33$ of $61$ primes only. (d) The identity fibre at all $61$ primes is covered independently by Proposition~4.1 (exhaustive) plus the Lemma~4.3 inclusion (exact) plus the proof of Proposition~4.4 (read and re-derived); the auditor's direct level-$11$ enumeration covers only $4$ primes of $P_{10}$. (e) \textbf{Nothing is formalised beyond the scalar inputs.}
\item \textbf{Blindness is the producer's attestation} and cannot be verified by anyone else; the auditor's reading of the S--T logs came after its own counts at $p=127,137$.
\item \textbf{Deviations from the preregistration.} (1) The preregistration assigns the implementation to a Sonnet-tier agent; the producer was Opus. (2) The canonical-support pruning and capacity bound are the producer's own, checked by mode agreement. (3) The preregistered ``$<\to\le$'' control is provably inert at $k=10$ (run anyway, plus a rounding mutation, and at $k=7,8$). (4) The unpruned second pass stopped at $33$ of $61$ primes. (5) Proposition~1.4 was audited \emph{and} replaced by a computation, beyond the preregistration. (6) Kill criterion: the preregistration asks for throughput at $p=127$ and a projection; $p=127$ alone ($4.7$ s) does not fix the growth rate, so a four-prime fit ($127,131,163,199$) was used; the first (plain) implementation scaled about $p^{10}$ and projected about $230$ core-hours at $p=467$; the algorithm was changed \emph{before} the full run, checked against the original by mode agreement, and the full run took about $1.1$ core-hours (about $3.3$ core-hours in all, on two shared cores). (7) The repository guards \texttt{antigravity\_guard.py} and \texttt{test\_rigor\_guard.py} exit 1 on files elsewhere in the repository, and neither flags a LR-IND file (\texttt{data/logs/guard\_*.txt}); \texttt{pytest tests/} was not run.
\item \textbf{Pre-freeze files} (kept for the record, not relied on): \texttt{logs/control\_k8\_lift33.jsonl}, \texttt{logs/k10\_p127\_probe.json}, \texttt{logs/probe\_k10\_p163/199/263.json}, \texttt{logs/probe\_k10\_p127\_x11.json} (the generic $\times11$ cross-check at $p=127$: its binary was built from the same source as the frozen one, before the freeze).
\item \textbf{Scope.} $k=10$ only ($11$ runners). Counts are of classes under the stated symmetries; the S--T-convention tuple counts are given for $33$ primes in \texttt{summary.json}.
\item Not a physical claim of any kind.
\end{itemize}
