\begin{abstract}
\noindent B.~Peng \cite{peng} (arXiv:2610.32739, 23 Oct 2026, unrefereed) classifies the bistable networks among small two-species bimolecular mass-action networks with at most three directed reactions: $681$ orbits under species exchange, of which $27$ are bistable (two positive equilibria that are sinks), with rational witnesses (his Table~2) and $654$ exclusions (JAC $245$, DIV $216$, ROOT $179$, FLUX $14$). \textbf{The result is Peng's; we claim nothing new.} Two independent implementations reproduce the enumeration ($1351$ labelled edge sets, $681$ orbits), verify all $27$ witnesses exactly (each has exactly three positive equilibria: two sinks and a saddle), and reproduce all four exclusion counts; the ROOT count $179$ is reproduced only by the auditor, with the resultant taken at the actual degree of each polynomial (the producer's fixed-degree reading gave $105$). The exclusions are certificate checks conditional on the paper's Lemma~3 (pen-and-paper, not machine-checked); Theorem~2 is not verified. Random searches (Tier~\tX) found no bistable network among the $654$ excluded ones. Outcome: PARTIAL. Tier~\tC{} for the exact parts, \tX{} for searches, \tL{} for the paper's lemmas.
\end{abstract}

\section{The class and the enumeration (\tC{})}\label{sec:enum}
Following Peng, the complexes are $X,Y,2X,X+Y,2Y$ and a network is a set of at most three distinct directed edges $\alpha\to\beta$ between them with $\alpha\ne\beta$ ($20$ possible edges). With mass action, $H=\sum_j k_j(\beta_j-\alpha_j)z^{\alpha_j}$ and the dynamics are
$z'=(a,b)-z+H$ (model E) or $z'=(a,b)-\mathrm{diag}(\rho,1)z+H$ (model F), all of $a,b,k_j,\rho>0$. The target (``bistable'') is: two distinct positive equilibria with $\operatorname{tr}DF<0$ and $\det DF>0$ (sinks). The paper uses $\mathrm{E}$ with $\rho=1$ for the witnesses.

Both implementations (own code each) find $1351$ labelled edge sets, of which $11$ are fixed by exchanging $X$ and $Y$, giving $681$ orbits of sizes $1/10/100/570$. Numbering the orbits by first appearance with the complexes and edge sets in lexicographic order reproduces all $27$ identifiers of the paper's Table~1, and the $27$ sets lie in $27$ distinct orbits (\texttt{BIST-C-0001}). The paper does not state the representative rule; first appearance reproduces it. The paper is internally inconsistent on the label count: Sec.~1 gives $1351$, Sec.~5 says ``all $1362$ labels'' ($1362=1351+11$); we take $1351$, which both implementations obtain.

\section{The 27 witnesses (\tC{})}\label{sec:wit}
Producer: for each Table~2 parameter set, Gr\"obner elimination of $y$, square-free resultant, exact Sturm count of positive roots, $y>0$ and the signs of trace and determinant by rational intervals of width $10^{-40}$ (gcd of the polynomial and the trace/determinant polynomial being $1$). Auditor, by a different method: the exact resultant in $y$, its square-free part, certified root isolation with Arb ball arithmetic (python-flint, $400$ bits), both $y$-roots of the second equation in ball arithmetic, candidates discarded when the first equation is certified nonzero. Result in both: \emph{every one of the $27$ has exactly three positive equilibria, two sinks and one saddle ($\det<0$), with no undecided sign} (\texttt{BIST-C-0002}). The $x$-coordinates agree (e.g.\ N0438: $0.020611$, $2.02$, $397.909$).

The Table~2 values were first read by the producer from flattened PDF text; four rows were mis-grouped and fixed by testing all groupings. The auditor then confirmed all $27$ rows against the rendered PDF image (the author's catalogue was not used). Rows N0514, N0524: $k_1=17$, $k_2=100/9$; rows N0548, N0554: $k_1=17$, $k_2=100/159$; $k_3=2600/2809,\,650/729,\,2600/2809,\,2600/2809$ respectively.

\section{The 654 exclusions (\tC{}, conditional on \tL{})}\label{sec:excl}
The paper excludes bistability by four certificate families, each valid on the paper's Lemma~3 (projection injectivity, ``at least three equilibria for two sinks'', and a Sard argument for the saddle) and, for JAC, a midpoint identity. We read these arguments; they look correct, but they are pen-and-paper and neither of us machine-checked them (\tL{}). What we checked is that the certificates exist for the claimed networks.

Rule & paper & reproduced & by\\ \midrule
JAC ($\det DF$ has nonnegative coefficients and the monomial $\rho$ with coefficient 1) & 245 & 245 & producer, auditor\\
DIV ($\det K$ nonnegative, monomial $ab$ with coefficient 1), after JAC & 216 & 216 & producer, auditor\\
ROOT (Sylvester resultant sign pattern) & 179 & 179 & auditor (producer: 105)\\
FLUX (copositivity on the flux cone) & 14 & 14 & auditor only\\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Exclusion counts; total $245+216+179+14=654$. None of the $27$ positives is excluded by any rule.}\label{tab:rules}
\end{table}
\paragraph{JAC and DIV.} Applied in that order, both implementations obtain $245$ and $216$, split by network size ($0$ edges $1/0$, $1$ edge $8/2$, $2$ edges $54/33$, $3$ edges $182/181$) exactly as in the paper's table; $193$ negatives remain, including all $14$ FLUX networks.
\paragraph{ROOT.} The auditor takes the Sylvester resultant at the actual degree of each polynomial in the eliminated variable, eliminating $x$ or $y$, with the paper's sign-pattern criterion (each coefficient fixed strict or free in $\{-1,0,1\}$, at most two sign variations, at least one fixed strict coefficient). This passes $179$ of the $193$: $13$ with two edges and $166$ with three, equal to the paper's per-size counts; $0$ positives and $0$ FLUX networks pass (eliminating $y$ alone gives $171$, $x$ alone $126$, union $179$). The producer, reading ``nominal degree'' as a fixed degree-$2$ ($4\times4$) Sylvester matrix, obtained only $105$. The auditor reproduces $105$ under that reading, so the gap of $88$ comes from the producer's reading, not from the paper; the actual-degree reading is also sound, since a fixed Sylvester determinant vanishes at every common root even under degree drop and the strict coefficient excludes the zero polynomial.
\paragraph{FLUX.} The auditor enumerated the rays of the flux cone $\{w\ge0,U\ge0\}$ ($6$ to $9$ per network) and formed $G=R^{\!\top}HR$, checking $w^{\top}Hw=\det M(w)$ symbolically. Eleven networks have $G$ entrywise nonnegative; N0391 gives $u^2-2uv+4v^2$ and N0646 gives $u^2-uv+v^2$ (both positive definite); N0610 gives $u^2+3uv+2v^2-uw-vw+w^2$, copositive but not positive semidefinite, with the paper's decomposition $(w-\tfrac{u+v}2)^2+\tfrac34u^2+\tfrac52uv+\tfrac74v^2$ checked by hand expansion. So FLUX $14/14$ reproduces (\texttt{BIST-C-0003}).

\section{Controls (\tC{})}\label{sec:ctrl}
Positive: the Sec.~4 baseline ($Y\to2X$ with $k=10$, $2X\to X+Y$ with $k=50/9$, $X+Y\to Y$ with $k=10$; $a=4/99$, $b=1/90$) has exactly the equilibria $x=1/10,\,1/5,\,3/5$ with $\det=25/9,\,-20/9,\,100/9$ (exact substitution) and trace formula as in the paper; as $a$ is raised, two sinks at $a=0.04610$ and one at $a=0.04611$, consistent with the fold at $a_+\approx0.0461033$. Negative: the empty network, $X\to Y$, $X\leftrightarrow Y$ and $X+Y\leftrightarrow2X$ each give exactly one positive equilibrium (a sink); the N0056 network ($X\to X+Y$, $X+Y\to2X$) gives exactly one at the producer's tested parameters but can give none (e.g.\ $a=1/3$, $b=2/7$, $k=3/2$, $h=5/4$, $\rho=1$), consistent with the paper's bound of at most two. Perturbation: scaling the inflow $a$ by $2,5,20,100,1000$ destroys bistability for all $27$ witnesses (\texttt{BIST-C-0004}).

\section{Random search (\tX{})}\label{sec:search}
Producer: $125{,}000$ log-uniform samples of $(a,b,\rho,k)$ in $[10^{-3},10^{3}]$ for each of the $681$ orbits ($85.1$M samples), every hit re-verified exactly in rationals: all $27$ positives re-found (hit rates $0.34\%$ to $8.4\%$); $0$ candidates among the $654$ negatives. Auditor, independent pipeline: $20{,}000$ samples for each of the $27$ positives and $193$ ROOT/FLUX negatives ($4.4$M): all $27$ re-found (lowest rate $0.39\%$); the float pipeline flagged $247$ samples on $10$ negative networks, all rejected by exact Arb re-check (none with two sinks, none undecided). Hence no bistable negative was found. This is evidence only, limited by the parameter box and sampling, and the producer's ``$0$ candidates'' depends on its own pipeline (\texttt{BIST-X-0001}).

\section{Limitations and what is not done}\label{sec:lim}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item AI audit only; no human audit; no Lean (no Tier~A).
\item Not verified: Peng's Lemma~3 and its Sard step, the JAC midpoint identity (read, not machine-checked), Theorem~2 (deletion-minimality and inheritance), and the claim $B_E=B_F$ beyond the counts. The $654$ exclusions are reproduced as certificate checks, not as a proof independent of Lemma~3.
\item The author's repository, supplementary certificates and checker were not used (repository URL not in the paper; licence unchecked).
\item Search limits: parameter box $[10^{-3},10^3]$, finite samples; model E only at $\rho=1$ for the witnesses.
\item Literature: no independent verification or critique of the paper and no earlier enumeration of this exact class was found, but the search was limited (\tL{}, \texttt{BIST-L-0002}); related work includes Tang and Xu \cite{tangxu}, not examined here beyond the abstract.
\item Trusted components: sympy, python-flint (Arb), Python \texttt{fractions}.
\end{itemize}
