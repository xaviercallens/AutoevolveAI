\begin{abstract}
\noindent Das and Fox (arXiv:2610.39032, 28 Oct 2026) claim a proof of the Murty--Simon conjecture: a diameter-2-critical (D2C) graph on $n$ vertices has at most $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges. Two of their lemmas are unconditional statements about all D2C graphs: Lemma~1.2 (for every maximal matching $M$, $|E\setminus M|$ is at most the number of non-edges) and Lemma~1.3 (if equality holds for every maximal matching, the graph is triangle-free). \textbf{The proof is theirs; we claim nothing new and do not verify it.} We test the two lemma statements, and Theorem~1.1, on every D2C graph with $3\le n\le 11$: $4{,}419$ graphs and $776{,}217$ maximal matchings, with exact integer arithmetic and two independent implementations (a C filter with Python, and a Rust filter with a full fresh enumeration of all $1{,}006{,}700{,}565$ connected graphs on $11$ vertices). There are no violations. The D2C counts equal OEIS A330333, so the census is a recomputation; Theorem~1.1 was already reported proved for $n\le 24$ and $n=26$ (Fan 1987, as reported by Dailly--Foucaud--Hansberg), so this is a sanity check, not new evidence. Tier~\tC{} throughout.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\fbox{\parbox{0.96\linewidth}{\small\textbf{What this is NOT.} Not a verification of the Das--Fox proof; not evidence for any $n\ge12$; not a new enumeration or new count; not a formal proof (no Lean); the intermediate lemmas (2.4, 2.5, 2.13, 3.1, 3.2) were checked only by the producer; not human-audited.}}

\section{Statements tested}\label{sec:def}
A graph is \emph{diameter-2-critical} (D2C) if it has diameter exactly $2$ and deleting any edge destroys this ($K_n$ is excluded). A \emph{maximal matching} is a matching to which no edge can be added. A \emph{non-edge} is a pair of distinct non-adjacent vertices. As read by the auditor from the full text of Das--Fox \cite{df} (\tL{}, \texttt{MS-L-0001}):
\begin{description}\itemsep=1pt
\item[Lemma 1.2.] For every D2C graph $G$ on $n\ge3$ vertices and every maximal matching $M$: $|E(G)\setminus M|\le|\overline{E}(G)|$.
\item[Lemma 1.3.] If equality holds for \emph{every} maximal matching $M$, then $G$ is triangle-free.
\item[Theorem 1.1.] A D2C graph on $n$ vertices has at most $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ edges.
\end{description}
Both lemmas carry no extra hypothesis, so they are directly testable on a finite range. Attribution, from the paper's AI statement: GPT-5.6 Sol found a proof of the $\lfloor n^2/4\rfloor$ bound (Lemma 1.2) and an equality-case proof for even $n$ only; the equality-case proof for all $n$ (Lemma 1.3) is the authors' own.

\section{Census and lemma test, $3\le n\le 11$ (\tC{})}\label{sec:res}
Producer: \texttt{nauty-geng -c} piped into a C filter (\texttt{d2c\_filter.c}), then Python (\texttt{lemmas.py}) for maximal matchings and lemma checks. Auditor, without running producer code: fresh \texttt{geng}, a Rust filter using a common-neighbour characterisation of edge criticality (cross-checked by explicit BFS edge deletion for $n\le10$, $0$ disagreements on $11{,}989{,}762$ connected graphs), a full fresh run over all $1{,}006{,}700{,}565$ connected graphs at $n=11$, and its own maximal-matching enumerator (equal to brute force over all edge subsets on the $51$ D2C graphs with $n\le8$ plus $14$ control graphs). Canonical forms (\texttt{labelg}) of the two D2C sets are equal for every $n=3,\dots,11$: symmetric difference $0$ (\texttt{MS-C-0001}, \texttt{-0002}).

\paragraph{Lemma 1.3 is nearly vacuous here.} Exactly one graph per $n$ is an equality case for \emph{all} maximal matchings: the balanced $K_{a,b}$, which is triangle-free. The auditor's additional finding: equality for even \emph{one} maximal matching occurs only at $K_{a,b}$. D2C graphs containing a triangle ($1,4,20,87,488,3685$ graphs for $n=6,\dots,11$) stay strictly below the bound of Lemma 1.2, by at least $1$ at $n=6$ and at least $10$ at $n=11$. Lemma~1.2 on $776{,}217$ maximal matchings is the informative test.

\paragraph{Controls.} Positive: C$_4$, C$_5$, Petersen, $K_{2,3}$, $K_{3,3}$, $K_{4,5}$, $K_{1,5}$ accepted by both D2C filters; negative: $K_3,K_4,K_5$, $K_4{-}e$, C$_6$, C$_7$, P$_4$ rejected. Connected-graph counts equal A001349; networkx's graph atlas reproduces the D2C counts for $n\le7$. Perturbed statements that must fail do fail: strict ``$<$'' in Lemma~1.2, the bound minus one, and the empty (non-maximal) matching all fail at $K_{a,b}$.

\paragraph{Structural facts of Dailly--Foucaud--Hansberg.} On the auditor's D2C list we reproduce: $13$ non-bipartite D2C graphs at $\lfloor (n-1)^2/4\rfloor+1$ edges outside the C$_5^+$ family (orders $7,8,9$: $3,8,2$ graphs; $3$ without a dominating edge); $10$ graphs at that bound with a dominating edge, all with $n\le9$; only $H_5$ ($n=6$, $8$ edges) above that bound; maximum degree $n-1$ only in stars (\tC{}, \texttt{MS-C-0003}).

\paragraph{Intermediate lemmas (\tX{}).} Lemmas 2.4, 2.5, 2.13, 3.1, 3.2 of the paper, as implemented by the producer from the paper's definitions (critical non-edge, $I$, $I'$, $C_\ell$), have $0$ violations on all $4{,}419$ graphs. The auditor did not recompute them (\texttt{MS-X-0001}).

\section{Prior art and strength of the evidence}\label{sec:lit}
The D2C counts $1,2,3,5,10,30,103,519,3746$ are OEIS A330333 (Zivkovic \cite{oeis}, Dec 2019; $a(11)$ added by S.~A.~Irvine, 5 Sep 2026): \url{https://oeis.org/A330333} (\tL{}, \texttt{MS-L-0002}). Dailly--Foucaud--Hansberg \cite{dfh} report a computer search over all graphs of order $\le11$ but publish no per-order counts; MacDougall--Eggleton (1997) list the D2C graphs up to $n=7$ (via the auditor). The census is therefore a recomputation. Fan (1987) proved the Murty--Simon conjecture for $n\le24$ and $n=26$ (as reported by \cite{dfh}; Fan was not read), so our check of Theorem~1.1 re-confirms an established range. The only possibly new element is the per-graph test of Lemmas 1.2--1.3; we did not search whether anyone has run it, and it is weak evidence about the proof.
