\begin{abstract}
The \emph{shifted} lonely runner conjecture (Blanco--Criado--Santos, arXiv:2603.24784, Conjecture~1.1(ii)) says: for distinct positive integer velocities $v_1,\dots,v_n$ and any real shifts $s_i$ there is a time $t$ with $\dist(s_i+v_it,\Z)\ge\frac1{n+1}$ for all $i$. It is known to fail for $5\le n\le17$ and $n\ge95$ (Poliakova, arXiv:2609.23952), and in our earlier note (doi:10.5281/zenodo.23842167) for $18\le n\le38$; the range $39\le n\le94$ was open.
\textbf{We give an explicit exact certificate that the shifted conjecture fails at $n=63$}: $63$ distinct positive integer velocities (largest $128$) and rational shifts $a_i/520$ whose $63$ open arcs of radius $\frac1{64}$ cover the circle. The exact value is $\gamma(v;s)=66/4225<\frac1{64}$ (gap $\frac1{270400}$), so $E_{63}\ge\frac1{66}$ in Poliakova's notation, and no more. The certificate is a Poliakova-type move on the base $2,4,\dots,128$: six even velocities removed, seven rephased, five odd velocities $1,3,9,27,81$ added; it is \emph{sporadic}: the same menu is infeasible for $N=n+1=65,\dots,95$ and $60,\dots,63$.
The certificate passed three exact checkers (two by the producer, one written independently by an auditing AI agent); the covering statement (with injectivity and positivity of the velocities) was kernel-checked in Lean~4 on a pinned toolchain with axioms within the standard whitelist, after a first compile failed on one auxiliary lemma and was repaired (Section~\ref{sec:lean}); no statement lock and no person's check of the statement exist. \textbf{This concerns the shifted conjecture at $n=63$ only; nothing is said about the unshifted lonely runner conjecture, and no other $n$ in $39..94$ is settled.}
\end{abstract}

\section{Statement and definition}\label{sec:setting}
\paragraph{The shifted conjecture.} (BCS \cite{bcs}, Conj.~1.1(ii); definition as pinned in our earlier note, which read it on the sources \cite{bcs,zhang,pol}.) For $n\ge1$, take $n$ runners with \emph{distinct positive integer} velocities $v_1,\dots,v_n$ and shifts $s_1,\dots,s_n\in\R/\Z$, and put
\[
\gamma(v;s)=\max_{t}\ \min_{1\le i\le n}\ \|s_i+v_it\|,\qquad \|x\|=\dist(x,\Z).
\]
The conjecture asserts $\gamma(v;s)\ge\frac1{n+1}$ for all such $(v,s)$. A counterexample of size $n$ is therefore a pair with $\gamma(v;s)<\frac1{n+1}$. For integer $v$ the function $t\mapsto\min_i\|s_i+v_it\|$ is $1$-periodic and continuous, so its maximum is attained, and
\[
\gamma(v;s)<\delta\iff\text{the open arcs }\{t\in\R/\Z:\ \|s_i+v_it\|<\delta\}\text{ cover }\R/\Z .
\]
A single uncovered point (tangency) is \emph{not} a cover. With $\delta=\frac1{n+1}$ this is the criterion every checker below uses. Distinctness is essential (with repeated velocities the conjecture fails trivially). In Poliakova's notation \cite{pol}, $\frac1{n+1+E_n}=\inf_{v,s}\gamma(v;s)$, so a certificate gives $E_n\ge1/\gamma(v;s)-(n+1)$.

\paragraph{Known ranges} (\tL{}, \texttt{SLR63-L-0001}). Fails for $5\le n\le17$ \cite{bcs} and $n\ge95$ \cite{pol}; proved for $n\le4$; for $18\le n\le38$ by our earlier note \cite{slr18}; Zhang \cite{zhang} lists the middle range as open. We make no claim beyond $n=63$.

\section{Exact verification}\label{sec:verify}
\paragraph{Three exact checkers (Tier \tC{}).}
\emph{Checker~1} (\texttt{slr\_exact.py}, Python integers; producer): every open-arc endpoint scaled to an integer, greedy sweep of open integer intervals; COVERED. \emph{Checker~2} (\texttt{check\_cover.c}, \texttt{\_\_int128}; producer): the arcs cover iff every arc endpoint lies strictly inside another arc; COVERED, $3961$ endpoints checked. These two share an author. \emph{Checker~3} (\texttt{audit63.py}, Python standard library plus numpy for a float pre-filter, written from scratch by the auditing AI agent without reading the producer's checkers): exact interval union in integer arithmetic over open intervals with wraparound, and the exact value of $\gamma$. A fourth script (\texttt{verify63.py}, the main session's own Fractions code, $7858$ distinct arc endpoints, none outside every open arc) agrees. We re-ran checker~3 and \texttt{verify63.py} for this paper (\texttt{data/logs/audit63\_run.txt}, \texttt{verify63\_run.txt}).
\paragraph{Exact value.} Among the $331632$ pairwise crossing candidates $s_i+v_it=\pm(s_j+v_jt)$, the maximum of $\min_i\|s_i+v_it\|$ is, evaluated with exact fractions,
\[
\gamma(v;s)=\frac{66}{4225}=\frac{66}{65^2}\approx0.0156213\quad\text{at }t^\ast=\frac{4291}{8450},\qquad \frac1{64}-\gamma=\frac1{270400}\approx3.7\times10^{-6}.
\]
(The sampled value $0.0156212$ in the producer's first report is superseded by this exact one.) The smallest endpoint slack over all arcs is about $3.76\times10^{-6}$.
\paragraph{Consequence.} $1/\gamma=4225/66=64+\frac1{66}$, hence $E_{63}\ge\frac1{66}$. \textbf{This is all the certificate gives}: it is not $E_{63}\ge1$ and says nothing about $\gamma_{\rm sh}$ beyond $\gamma_{\rm sh}(v)\le\gamma(v;s)<\frac1{64}$.
\paragraph{Negative and tightness checks.} The cover \emph{fails} at radius $r=\gamma$ (tangent: an uncovered point exists), holds at $\gamma+10^{-12}$, and fails at $r=\frac1{65}$ (uncovered point at $t\equiv\frac1{130}$, so the certificate is not trivially loose). Replacing velocity $81$ by $27$ (duplicate) is rejected as non-distinct. Perturbing each shift by $\pm\frac1{2D}$ breaks the cover in $83$ of $126$ cases (auditor); the producer's smaller perturbation $\pm\frac1{10\sum v}$ broke $3$ of $126$. With $s=0$ and velocities $\{1,\dots,63\}$ the cover is rejected, as it must be.

\section{Structure: a Poliakova-type move}\label{sec:structure}
Poliakova \cite{pol} starts from $2,4,\dots,2N$ at shift $0$ with $N=n+1$ and radius $\frac1{N+1}$; at an odd scale $b$ coprime to $6$ she removes $8b,32b,192b$, rephases $12b,24b,48b,96b$ to $\frac{1\pm\delta}2$ and $16b,64b$ to $\mp\delta$ and adds $3b,9b$ at $\frac14$; the largest scaled velocity $192b\le2N$ forces $n\ge95$. (Her Section~3 and Table~1 differ in the signs for $48,96,64$; both variants cover at $n=95..109$ in our check, \texttt{data/code/rule.py}.)

Our certificate has the same shape for $N=64$ on the base $2,4,\dots,128$ at the \emph{open} radius $\frac1{64}$ (which has slack over her closed radius $\frac1{65}$):
\begin{center}\small\begin{tabular}{ll}\toprule
removed (even, from shift $0$) & $4,8,32,72,96,108$\\
rephased (kept, shift changed) & $12,24,36\to\frac{1+e}2$; $48\to\frac{1-e}2$; $16,128\to-e$; $64\to+e$\\
added (odd) & $1,3,9,27,81$ at about $\frac34$\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{center}
Here the largest rephased velocity is $128=2N$ instead of $192$, and odd powers of $3$ near shift $\frac34$ fill the remaining gaps. The count is $64-6=58$ even runners plus $5$ odd ones $=63=n$.
\paragraph{How it was found.} A CP-SAT set cover (\texttt{cpsat\_rule.py}): base $2,\dots,2N$ at shift $0$, radius $\frac1N$; a pool of $3$-smooth multiples of $4$ and $\{3,5\}$-smooth odd velocities may be removed, rephased or added, with shifts $\frac c4+\frac j{8(N+1)}$, $|j|\le8$, and at most (pool evens)$-1$ changed runners; exact integer test points (all arc endpoints and cell midpoints inside the part left uncovered by the fixed runners) feed the set cover. Positive control: with Poliakova's rule fixed at $N=96$ ($n=95$) the model is feasible and the extracted certificate is COVERED by both producer checkers.
\paragraph{It is sporadic.} The \emph{same} pool and menu are infeasible (CP-SAT, exact for that pool and menu) for $N=95,\dots,65$ ($N=72$ needed $600$ s) and for $N=63,\dots,60$; only $N=64$ is feasible. Why it does not extend uniformly is not understood and no general rule was proved. These infeasibility statements concern only that pool, menu and $N$; they say nothing about the conjecture, and they were \textbf{not re-run by the auditor}. With a wider menu ($J=16$) infeasibility was found for $N=47,46,44$ and the solver returned UNKNOWN at $120$ s for the other tried values; larger pools (5-smooth, all multiples of $4$) returned UNKNOWN at $120$--$400$ s (logs in \texttt{data/logs/}; Tier~\tX{}). Random local search from an even base found no certificate (best uncovered measure $\approx10^{-4}$ at several $n$).
\paragraph{Phase-2 evidence (Tier~\tX{}/\tC{}; \texttt{data/RESULT\_PHASE2.md}).} A second round looked for further members of the family. Within the pools and shift menu tried (shifts $\frac c4+\frac j{8(N+1)}$, $|j|\le8$; pools: $3$-smooth multiples of $4$ with $\{3,5\}$-smooth odds, $5$-smooth, and $3$-smooth evens with $\{3,5,7\}$-smooth odds), $N=64$ ($n=63$) is the only $N$ in $39..95$ that admits a cover. The smooth pool with $J=8$ is infeasible for every other $N$ in $39..95$; the $5$-smooth pool is infeasible at $N=47,46,45,43,39$ and unknown (time limit $120$ s) at the remaining values tried; the larger $3$-smooth-even pool timed out (even the positive control at $N=64$). A direct application of the $n=63$ move with $\delta=1/(N+k)$, $k=-1..2$, for $N=64..129$ covers only at $N=64$, so the move is not uniform in $N$. This is evidence, not a theorem: unknown cases remain and none of it was re-run by the auditor. The same search also returned a second valid $n=63$ solution, \texttt{data/certificates/cert\_n063\_alt\_sw3\_p5.json} (sha256 \texttt{dfc8b4d79664a48053aff2b1e3d057235ced913cea483e46b5484bc8fac68d7a}), accepted by both producer checkers (COVERED, $3961$ endpoints); it is \textbf{not} audited and \textbf{not} Lean-checked, and nothing here depends on it. Phase 2 used about $8$ core-hours, about $24$ in total.

\section{Lean}\label{sec:lean}
The generated file \texttt{data/lean/SLRN63.lean} (imports \texttt{Mathlib.Tactic} only, never \texttt{import Mathlib}; sha256 in \texttt{BUNDLE\_MANIFEST.txt}) states, for the typed $v:\mathrm{Fin}\,63\to\mathbb N$ and $s:\mathrm{Fin}\,63\to\mathbb Q$ (the certificate's $a_i/520$):
\begin{lstlisting}
theorem v_injective : Function.Injective v := by decide
theorem v_pos : ∀ i, 0 < v i := by decide
theorem cover : ∀ t : ℝ, ∃ i : Fin 63, ∃ k : ℤ,
    |((s i : ℚ) : ℝ) + ((v i : ℕ) : ℝ) * t - (k : ℝ)| < 1 / 64
\end{lstlisting}
\lean{cover} is exactly the negation of the conclusion of the conjecture for this $(v,s)$; with injectivity and positivity of $v$ it is a counterexample of size $63$. It needs neither $\gamma$ nor compactness.
\paragraph{What happened, in order.} (1) A first full compile of the unmodified file (auditor, pinned Lean~4.34.1 toolchain, $3304$ s) returned \textbf{rc~1}: \lean{cover} was kernel-accepted (axioms \lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound}), \lean{v\_pos} accepted (\lean{propext}), but \lean{v\_injective} failed (\texttt{decide}: maximum recursion depth reached) and so depended on \lean{sorryAx}; the file as a whole did not pass (\texttt{data/lean/compile\_first\_attempt.log}). (2) The fix is \texttt{set\_option maxRecDepth 1000000 in} before \lean{v\_injective}. A small-file test containing $v$, $s$ and the two small theorems returned rc~0 in $13.6$ s with axioms \lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound} for \lean{v\_injective} and \lean{propext} for \lean{v\_pos} (\texttt{data/lean/lean\_inj.json}). (3) The full recompile of the fixed file (sha256 in \texttt{BUNDLE\_MANIFEST.txt}) returned \textbf{rc~0} in $3327.6$ s through \texttt{scripts/certified\_numerics/lean\_pinned.py} (compile plus \texttt{\#print axioms}; \texttt{data/lean/lean\_full.json}, \texttt{data/lean/compile\_full.log}). Axioms: \lean{cover}: \lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound}; \lean{v\_injective}: the same three; \lean{v\_pos}: \lean{propext}. No error is reported, and the source contains no \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, \texttt{native\_decide} or \texttt{axiom}. (4) A second, independent run of the same compile (same runner \texttt{lean\_pinned.py}, same pinned toolchain; the file regenerated from the certificate copy by the same generator and byte-identical to \texttt{data/lean/SLRN63.lean}, sha256 \texttt{090944f2\ldots}) returned \textbf{rc~0} in $2028.2$ s with identical axioms (\texttt{data/lean/lean\_second.json}, \texttt{data/lean/compile\_second.log}). Both full compiles were run by the main session: the second is a repeat, \emph{not} an independent auditor's compile and \emph{not} a run through \texttt{anse/formal/lean\_runner.py}. The first attempt's failure and its repair are part of the record, not hidden.

\paragraph{Final gate status.} Passed for the compile-and-axioms gate used here, with these limits stated plainly. (a) The compile used the \texttt{certified\_numerics} runner \texttt{lean\_pinned.py}, \emph{not} \texttt{anse/formal/lean\_runner.py}. (b) The statement is \emph{not} locked by LeanMaster's statement-lock tool. (c) The Lean file was produced by our untrusted generator; this does not weaken the kernel result, because the kernel re-checks every case of \lean{cover}, and distinctness (\lean{v\_injective}) and positivity (\lean{v\_pos}) of $v$ are theorems in the same file. (d) A kernel-checked statement certifies that statement, not its gloss: the claim is the stated covering of the circle by the $63$ open arcs, nothing beyond it.

\medskip\noindent The Lean covering statement is filed at Tier~\tA{} as \texttt{SLR63-A-0001} (with an \texttt{ai\_audit} field; the gate field \texttt{audit} stays null). \section{Limitations}\label{sec:lim}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep=1pt
\item \textbf{Shifted conjecture only.} Nothing here concerns the unshifted lonely runner conjecture. One value of $n$ ($63$); no other $n$ in $39..94$ is settled, and no uniform rule was found.
\item \textbf{$E_{63}\ge\frac1{66}$ only}, an upper bound $\Gamma(63)<\frac1{64}$; no exact $\gamma_{\rm sh}$.
\item \textbf{Sporadic.} Infeasible for the same pool at $N=65..95$ and $60..63$; why is not understood.
\item \textbf{AI producer and AI auditor; no human audit}, no statement lock.
\item \textbf{Negative sweeps unverified by the auditor}: the CP-SAT infeasibility runs were not re-run, nor \texttt{cpsat\_rule.py 64 8 60}. The Poliakova rule was re-derived from the paper text by the producer and auditor only.
\item \textbf{Literature search} covers alphaXiv and one web search; novelty is ``to our knowledge'' only. Premises about prior ranges are taken as stated (\tL{}).
\item \textbf{Lean:} the first full compile failed on \lean{v\_injective} (fixed by \texttt{set\_option maxRecDepth 1000000}); the full recompile passed (rc~0, axioms whitelisted) and a second run of the same compile (same runner and file, main session, $2028.2$ s) agreed; it was not compiled by an independent auditor. Not run through \texttt{anse/formal/lean\_runner.py}; no LeanMaster statement lock; generated by an untrusted generator (the kernel re-checks every case); no person has certified that the statement means what its gloss says.
\item \textbf{Phase-2 evidence} is a set of exact infeasibility results for specific pools and a shift menu plus UNKNOWN cases; it supports no claim beyond them. The alternative $n=63$ solution is unaudited and not Lean-checked.\item \textbf{Compute.} About $15$--$16$ core-hours in phase 1 and about $8$ in phase 2 (about $24$ in all; upper bound; CP-SAT counted as wall$\times6$ or $7$), cores $1$--$7$; a host reboot occurred during the run and the final sweep completed.
\end{itemize}
