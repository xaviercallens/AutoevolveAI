The honest audit that started it

The previous "multi-agent swarm" (bsd_agent_swarm.py) contained agents that were asyncio.sleep stubs returning canned dictionaries; its success rates were fake by construction. This run replaced it with real execution, and the gate itself was tested before anything else was trusted.

Gate hole found and fixed. A proof from axiom cheat : False compiled with exit 0 and passed the old gate. lean_runner.py now whitelists exactly {propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound} and rejects everything else. sorry also exits 0 — only the #print axioms check catches it. Vacuous statements (True := trivial) still need statement-level review.

Top 3 selected approaches

1 · The computational–formal ladder (H3 + H6)

PARI/Sage computes ground truth (ranks, L-values); Lean kernel-checks the discrete facts (points on curves, torsion, equation membership). Cheapest verified result per token, and it scales curve by curve. Next rungs: torsion subgroup lemmas, 2-descent certificates, more of Cremona's tables as formal statements.

2 · DeepSeek-Prover behind the hardened gate (H1 + H4)

The pipeline prover is DeepSeek-Prover-V2-7B on the T4, every output through lake env lean + axiom whitelist. Follow-up: apply Goedel-Prover's documented chat template — its 0/9 is an artifact, and it may be the stronger model once prompted correctly.

3 · RAG-grounded proving (H2 + H8)

Premise retrieval from the 45,455-signature FLT corpus plus the literature collection, injected into prover prompts. The next experiment that matters: proof-rate with vs. without retrieved premises on a fixed statement set.
