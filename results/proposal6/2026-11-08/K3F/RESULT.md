K3 paper: papers/p4_carbon_impulse_response/main.tex (doi 10.5281/zenodo.23848023). Proved: T2 (Tier C) single exponential IRF => u=h*m quasi-concave for weakly unimodal m (stated as special case of Ibragimov); T3/Cor (Tier C, certified instances) CM non-exponential IRF => explicit staircase window B/A in (h(d),L) gives two maxima. Open/limitation: which IRFs give "at most one maximum" beyond the exponential.
Follow-up answer is classical: Ibragimov 1956 (strong unimodality): convolution with every unimodal input stays unimodal IFF the kernel is log-concave. For a one-sided IRF h on [0,inf) (h=0 for t<0) this is: h log-concave on [0,inf) (a jump at 0 allowed). It is already necessary AND sufficient, so there is nothing left to find; K3 itself notes the CM non-exponential case is strictly log-convex (violates it).
