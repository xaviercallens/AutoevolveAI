Class: species X,Y,Z; C = {X,Y,Z,2X,2Y,2Z,X+Y,X+Z,Y+Z}; internal reaction = directed edge alpha->beta, alpha != beta, both in C; network = set of m<=3 edges; plus flows 0->S (a_S>0) and S->0 (rho_S>0) for each S. Mass action. Labelled count 62269 (m=0..3: 1,72,2556,59640).
Completeness: enumerate all labelled sets, canonicalize by min over the 6 species permutations, assert orbit sizes sum to 62269 and cross-check count by Burnside (~10.4k). Stratify networks not touching Z (or Y) as lower-species cases.
Target: two distinct positive equilibria, both hyperbolic sinks (Routh-Hurwitz a1>0, a3>0, a1*a2>a3 in exact rationals), same parameters.
Method: (1) witness search: Sobol/log-uniform rational parameters, exact steady states via Groebner/resultants (Singular, sympy, gp, sage all available), exact Hurwitz test, Sturm/Arb interval re-verification. (2) Exclusion certificates: JAC (Jacobian determinant has nonnegative coefficients with a strictly positive term => no two equilibria by midpoint identity, valid in any dimension for quadratic fields), DIV-style sign certificates, resultant/Groebner bounds on number of positive equilibria (<=2 forbids two sinks). Anything else = UNDECIDED, reported, never counted negative.
Positive control: same pipeline on Peng's 2-species alphabet reproduces 27 of 681 and the exclusion counts for each implemented rule (JAC 245, DIV 216, ROOT 179, FLUX 14); a Z-decoupled embedding of a Peng mechanism must be found bistable.
Negative control: empty network, a deficiency-zero network with flows, and N0056 (at most 2 positive equilibria) must be rejected; perturbation control: scaling inflows x1000 kills bistability of each witness.
Kill criteria: controls fail; after 5 core-hours more than 50% of orbits UNDECIDED; prior-art re-screen at freeze and before publication finds a 3-species bistability census; cap 15 core-hours on disjoint pinned cores.
