## STEP 2: feasibility
- Available: sage (/usr/bin/sage) with Arb balls (RealBallField/ComplexBallField work), mpmath 1.2.1, scipy 1.8, gp. NOT available: python-flint, vnodelp, capd, julia. Rust crates not checked for interval libs (no inari seen).
- So a hand-written validated method is needed. Robertson is favorable: 3 species, mass conservation y1+y2+y3=1 (2D), positive orthant invariant, long-time dissipative toward the slow manifold. Plan: Taylor series of high order in Arb with a-priori enclosure by Picard-Lindelof plus a log-norm (one-sided Lipschitz) contraction bound on the transversal fast direction, so no step restriction from stiffness (Neumaier-type). Honest difficulty: moderate-high (implementing and auditing a sound stepper is the real work; 1-2 agent-days). 10 core-hours is ample if it works (Arb arithmetic, ~1e4-1e5 steps) and useless if the enclosure blows up (wrapping through the fast mode). Reaching t=1e11 is doubtful; t up to 1e2..1e5 is plausible.
- Second system "plasma benchmark from rusty-SUNDIALS" is unspecified: drop or replace by VdP mu=100..1000 (2D, limit cycle; enclosure over many relaxation oscillations is much harder: expect blow-up), so not promised.

## STEP 3: verdict and claim
Phenomenon (BDF global error can exceed rtol, grows with t, esp. over long stiff integrations) is textbook; the only new thing would be a rigorous number. Value: modest; useful as a trust anchor for the Bari reference and for our CVODE/rusty-SUNDIALS use. Worth a short note only if the enclosure is genuinely narrow; otherwise a negative record.
Exact narrowed claim: "Validated enclosure of Robertson's problem (k=0.04, 3e7, 1e4; y(0)=(1,0,0)) on [0,T], T in {1e2..1e5, extended to 1e11 only if it holds}, width w(t); CVODE (rtol 1e-4..1e-10, atol 1e-8..1e-14, BDF, analytic Jacobian) true error E(t) in [a,b], exceeding rtol-scaled expectation by factor k at t*." Kill: enclosure width > 100x CVODE error at all t of interest, or soundness not auditable.
